Loving Father, we pray for so many people who are unhappy or lonely or ill.  Comfort and help them, we pray, especially those who are in hospitals, and all blind people and cripples.  Help them to realize that Jesus cares for them and can bring comfort and joy into their lives.  In his name we ask this.
