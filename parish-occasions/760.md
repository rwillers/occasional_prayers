Almighty God, who hast given to thy Son Jesus Christ the name which is above every name, and hast taught us that there is salvation in none other: Mercifully grant that as thy faithful people have comfort and peace in his name, they may ever labour to publish it to all nations; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
