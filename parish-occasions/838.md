O God, our Father, whose will is our peace: Strengthen and sustain the common life of this community.  Forgive our sins and negligences; exalt our purposes: purify our aims; free us from selfish desires; and make us of one heart and mind in thy service, for the glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
