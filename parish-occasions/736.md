Almighty and everlasting Lord, God of the spirits of all flesh: We commend to thy mercy the souls of our brothers whom we have remembered before thee; beseeching thee that the memory of their devotion may ever be an example and inspiration to us, and that we may serve thee faithfully all the days of our life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
