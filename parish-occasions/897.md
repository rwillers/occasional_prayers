We thank thee, heavenly Father, for giving us Christmas Day, and for all the joys of Christmas time.  Above all we thank thee for sending Jesus into the world on that first Christmas Day long ago.  Help us to keep his birthday by being thoughtful and loving for his sake, and by offering him the love of our hearts.  For his name’s sake.
