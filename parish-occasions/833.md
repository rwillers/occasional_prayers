Grant thy help, O Lord, to all who feel ill-equipped for the demands life makes upon them, whether through age or illness, tiredness or poverty.  Sustain them in overwork or anxiety.  Give them friends in their need, and enable them to find thy will in changed circumstances, and to rejoice in thy love even amid hardships and difficulty; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
