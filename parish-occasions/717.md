title: 'Education Sunday — For Homes and Parents'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 717
---

O God our Father, who hast made men to live together in families: Preserve to us the sanctities of family life; unite parents and children in true affection to one another in thy holy fear; and give wisdom to all Christian parents and teachers, that they may bring up children in true faith and in obedience to thy Word; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
