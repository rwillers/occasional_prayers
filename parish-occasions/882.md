Loving Father, bless our homes and our loved ones.  Help us to be grateful for all the good things we enjoy.  Keep us from grumbling and ill-temper.  Help us, with cheerful hearts, to be kind to one another and to do our share in making our home the abode of happiness and love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
