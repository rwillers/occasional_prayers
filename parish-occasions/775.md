O God, the author of all wisdom, who hast taught us that without love we can know nothing as we ought: Pour out thy Spirit of wisdom and love on all who are called to teach in this parish; that having the mind of Christ, they may serve the needs of others and give themselves wholly to the doing of thy will; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
