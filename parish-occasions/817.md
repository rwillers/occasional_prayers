---
title: 'Gift Day, or Stewardship Sunday — Christian Stewardship (3)'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 817
---

O God, whose blessed Son Jesus Christ earned his bread by the labours of his hands, and taught us that all things we possess are committed to us as a trust from thee: Help us to be faithful in the exercise of our stewardship; that in earning we may be just and honourable, and in spending we may seek first not our own indulgence but thy glory and the good of others; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
