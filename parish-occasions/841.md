We lift up our hearts, O Lord, in intercession for all who carry civic and political responsibilities.  Grant that, putting aside all merely selfish ambition, they may seek to be the instruments of thy will and carry out thy purpose for the welfare of thy people; and may they both seek and see thy glory in happier human lives; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
