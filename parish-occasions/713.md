Almighty and everlasting God, the giver of every good and perfect gift: Send thy blessing, we beseech thee, on all who teach in this school \[or in the schools of this parish\]; and so strengthen them by the grace of the Holy Spirit that they may build up in the faith and love of thy dear Son those committed to their care; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
