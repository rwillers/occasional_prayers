O God, who willest that all men should be saved and come to the knowledge of the truth: Prosper, we pray thee, our brethren who labour in distant lands \[especially those for whom our prayers are desired\].  Protect them in all perils; support them in loneliness and in the hour of trial; give them grace to bear faithful witness to thee; and endue them with burning zeal and love, that they may turn many to righteousness; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
