Almighty God, from whom cometh every good and perfect gift: Send down, we beseech thee, upon all those who hold office in this borough the spirit of justice and integrity, of wisdom and charity; that seeking their own good in the well-being of those committed to their charge, they may ever advance thy kingdom upon earth and promote the true welfare of thy people; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
