We thank thee, our Father, for the Bible, which teaches us about thee, and for thy servants of old who loved thy holy Word and wrote it down for us.  We thank thee that we can learn there about Jesus, our Friend and Saviour.  Help us to read and learn and understand, so that we may grow in the knowledge of thee.  For Jesus Christ’s sake.
