O God, we thank thee that thou hast called us into the fellowship of thy world-wide Church.  Unite us, and all its members everywhere, in love and loyalty to carry out thy purpose of love in the world today; and grant that we may not fail thee in this time of opportunity; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
