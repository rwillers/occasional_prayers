O God, who has taught us to live in the towns and cities of this world as knowing our citizenship to be in heaven: Bless the people of this borough, and guide with thy heavenly wisdom the mayor, aldermen, councillors, and those who bear office in the same; that they may ever keep before their eyes the vision of that city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
