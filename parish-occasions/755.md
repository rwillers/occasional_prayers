Thanks be unto thee, O God, for revealing thyself to man, and for sending forth thy messengers in every age.<br><br>Thanks be unto thee for the first apostles of Christ, sent forth into all the world to preach the gospel;<br>for those who brought the good news to our land;<br>for all who, in ages of darkness, kept alive the light, or in times of indifference were faithful to their Lord’s command;<br>for all thy followers in every age who have given their lives for the faith;<br>for those in our own day who have gone to the ends of the earth as heralds of thy love;<br>for the innumerable company who now praise thee out of every kindred and nation and tongue.<br>With these and the whole company of heaven we worship thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
