O Ever-blessed Jesus, very God and very Man, who wast thyself a child, and didst submit thyself to the charge and care of earthly teachers: Bless, we beseech thee, the children who art taught in our schools.  Grant that as they grow in years they may grow in knowledge of thyself; let them learn to love and serve thee truly, and daily order their lives according to thy will; for the glory of thy holy name.
