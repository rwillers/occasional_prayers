Almighty God, from whom cometh every good and perfect gift: Accept, we beseech thee, the offerings which thy people here present to thee with willing and thankful hearts; and grant us so to consecrate ourselves to thy service here, that we may glorify thee hereafter in thy heavenly kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
