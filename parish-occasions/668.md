O Lord Jesus Christ, who didst come to live among men as the child of a human mother: Bless us, we pray thee, as we gather in thy house today to worship thee and to thank thee for our mothers and our homes.  Fill our hearts with thy love, and help us to remember thy presence with us, not only now but at all times.  We ask it for thy name’s sake.
