Lord Jesus, who for thirty years didst dwell in thy humble home at Nazareth: Be with us, we pray thee, in our homes.  Keep from them all pride and selfishness, that they may be dwellings fit for thy presence, where we may learn to love and serve thee truly; for thy name’s sake.
