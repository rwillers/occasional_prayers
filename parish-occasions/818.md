O Lord Jesus Christ, who by thy incarnation didst sanctify material things to be the means of thy grace: Grant us a right understanding of the nature of money and a generous heart in the use of the wealth thou hast committed to our stewardship, that by our sacrifices we may glorify thee, who with the Father and the Holy Ghost art ever to be glorified.
