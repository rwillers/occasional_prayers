Give, O Lord, to all who till the ground, wisdom to understand, thy laws, and to cooperate with thy wise ordering of the world: and grant that the bountiful fruits of the earth may not be hoarded by the selfish or squandered by the foolish, but that all who work may share abundantly in the harvest of the soil; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
