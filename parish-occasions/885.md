O Lord our God, we thank thee for our country and for the freedom and peace we enjoy.  We pray thee to give wisdom to our Queen and to all who rule over us.  Make us as a nation strong in faith, that we may stand firm for truth and right.  So lead us and all nations in the way of peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
