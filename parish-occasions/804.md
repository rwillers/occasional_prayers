O Heavenly Father, who hast filled the world with beauty, open our eyes, we beseech thee, to behold thy gracious hand in all thy works; that rejoicing in thy whole creation, we may learn to serve thee with gladness of heart; for the sake of him by whom all things were made, thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
