O Lord our God, before whom the heavens do bow, who dost give ear to the praises of thy Church on earth: Look, we beseech thee, upon those who sing in this choir; give them reverence in worship, sincerity of purpose, and purity of life; that what they sing with their lips they may believe in their hearts and show forth in their lives, to the honour and glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
