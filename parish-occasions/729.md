Out of the darkness of our divided world we cry to thee, O God.  Let not the hopes of men perish, nor the sacrifice of men be in vain.  Turn to thyself the hearts of rulers and peoples, that a new world may arise where men may live as thy children in the bond of thy peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
