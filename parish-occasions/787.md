O God of truth, by whose Spirit we are taught knowledge and wisdom in all goodness: Grant thy blessing, we beseech thee, to all students in our universities and colleges.  Kindle and keep alive in their hearts the holy fire of faith and love, that they may set thy will ever before them, and consecrate their lives to thy service; for the glory of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
