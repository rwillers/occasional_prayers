O Lord Jesus Christ, who art the Saviour and Friend of age and youth: Look graciously, we beseech thee, upon those who give themselves to serve and help the young. Bless their works undertaken in thy name, and so inspire them with thy Holy Spirit that old and young may be builded together as living stones into the temple of thy Church; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, ever one God, world without end.
