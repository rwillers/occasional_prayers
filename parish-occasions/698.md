O God, who hast knit us together in a world family whose members live by giving and receiving the fruits of the earth according to their own and others’ needs: Grant that all nations of men who share the good gifts of thy bounty may look beyond the gifts to the Giver, and increase in the knowledge and love of thee, the Father of all; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
