Almighty Father, Lord of heaven and earth, be pleased to accept these gifts of thy children for the service of this House of Prayer; for all things come of thee and of thine own do we give thee.  Grant that what neither art nor costliness can make worthy for thy service may be hallowed by thy blessing; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
