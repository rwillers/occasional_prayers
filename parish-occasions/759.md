Lord of the harvest, the nations are waiting for thy message, and asking for messengers, and there are few who go, and few who give, and few who pray.  O grant that we may hear thy voice, and help in whatever way we can, by prayer, by gifts, and by service, to make thy gospel known to all the world; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Saviour.
