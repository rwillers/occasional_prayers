Let thy blessing rest, O God, upon this our nation; upon our Queen and all in authority under her.  Deliver us from all sin and corruption, from all weakness and fear; keep us faithful to the high trust thou hast laid upon us; and deepen our life in that righteousness which alone exalteth a nation; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
