Blessed Jesus, we rejoice today in thy mighty victory over sin and death.  Thou art the risen Lord, thou art the Prince of life, thou art alive for evermore.  We pray that we may know thy presence with us, not only as we worship thee here, but at home and school and all the days of our life; for thy great name’s sake.
