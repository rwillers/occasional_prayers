title: 'A Memorial Service — Those Who Mourn (2)'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 861
---

O Lord Jesus Christ, who by thy holy apostle hast bidden us not to be sorry as men without hope for them that sleep in thee: Visit, we beseech thee, with thy compassion those who mourn the loss of their beloved, and wipe away all tears from their eyes; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, ever one God, world without end.
