O God, from whom every good and perfect gift doth come: We thank thee for all the gifts and opportunities that are ours as young people; and we pray thee to give us grace to consecrate to thy service the talents which thou halt committed to our charge, and to use them always to thy glory; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
