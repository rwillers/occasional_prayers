O Lord, who hast taught us that in thy Father’s house are many mansions: We thank thee for the heavenly home which thou hast gone to prepare for us.  Prepare us, we pray thee, for that home, and in thy mercy bring us all at last to be partakers of its joys, that we may dwell with thee for evermore.
