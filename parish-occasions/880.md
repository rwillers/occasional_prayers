O Heavenly Father, we confess that we have sinned greatly against thee in thought, and word, and deed, and have done that which is wrong in thy sight.  We ask thee to forgive us our sins, and to give us strength day by day to resist temptation and to do thy will; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
