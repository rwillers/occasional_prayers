Almighty God, who alone givest wisdom and understanding: Inspire, we pray thee, the minds of all to whom thou hast committed the responsibility of government and leadership in the nations of the world.  Give to them the vision of truth and justice, that by their counsels all nations and classes may work together in true brotherhood, and serve thee in unity and peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
