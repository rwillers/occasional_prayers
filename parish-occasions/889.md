O God, we bring before thee the needs of children in other lands.  We pray for those who are oppressed and for those who are starving and homeless.  We remember the millions who know nothing of thy love and live in want and fear.  Bless our missionary doctors and teachers who go to help them, and may all children come to see in Jesus their Saviour and their Friend.
