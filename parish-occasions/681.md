title: 'Mothering Sunday Service — The Gift of Flowers'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 681
---

Bless, O Lord Jesus, these small gifts of flowers, that they may be channels of thy love, imparting joy to those who give and those who receive.  And we pray that all children and parents, united in thee, may ever follow thy example in loving words and deeds, to thy honour and glory, now and for evermore.
