Blessed Lord, who didst welcome the children who were brought to thee, and didst lay thine hands upon them and bless them: Forgive our neglect of those who know thee not, and inspire thy Church with new zeal to guide them into the knowledge of thy love, that they may know thee as their Friend and Saviour, and serve thee unto their lives’ end; for thy name’s sake.
