O God our heavenly Father, who art the giver of life and light: We beseech thee to help and inspire those who teach in the Sunday schools of this parish.  Pour out upon them the spirit of unceasing prayer and faithful service; encourage them with good success; strengthen their faith and purpose when they are weary and disheartened; and fortify them with the assurance that they are fellow workers with thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
