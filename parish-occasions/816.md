---
title: 'Gift Day, or Stewardship Sunday — Christian Stewardship (2)'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 816
---

Almighty God, who orders all things and has called us to thy service: Enable us to use wisely the time, ability and possessions entrusted to us, that we may be good and faithful servants, and may enter at last into the joy of our Lord; through the same our Saviour Jesus Christ.
