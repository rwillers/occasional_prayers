Almighty God our heavenly Father, who hast called us to the knowledge of thy grace, and faith in thee: Increase our faith, we beseech thee, as thou dost increase our years; and the longer we are suffered to abide on earth, the better may our service be, the more willing our obedience, the more consistent our daily living, the more complete our devotion to thee; through our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
