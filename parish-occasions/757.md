Almighty and everlasting God, whose beloved Son became man for us men and for our salvation, and gave commandment to his disciples that they should go and teach all nations, and baptize them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Ghost: Give us grace to be obedient to his command, and grant that all men may have new birth in him, and, being delivered out of the power of darkness, may be received into the kingdom of thy love: through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
