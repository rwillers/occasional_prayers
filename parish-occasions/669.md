O Heavenly Father, we thank thee for all the happiness of our life.  We thank thee for our baptism and for our place in thy family, the Church.  And especially today we thank thee for our parents, and for all those who have loved and cared for us.  Give us ever thankful and happy hearts, for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
