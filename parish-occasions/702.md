tags: [Various Occasions]

We commend to thy grace, O God, all who are entrusted with the promotion of sound education in this country.  Give wisdom to those who teach, that they may first be taught of thee; and grant to parents so to love thee that Christian homes may be the foundation of our common life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
