tags: [Various Occasions]

Lord Jesus Christ, who didst show on this earth thy love for children: Guide, we pray thee, with thy Spirit, those who are called to the ministry of teaching in this land; that nothing may hinder our children from growing in faith and love towards thee, and that thy name may be honoured both in our schools and in our homes.
