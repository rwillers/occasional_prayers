Almighty God our heavenly Father, whose Son Jesus Christ was subject to his parents in the home of Nazareth: Grant thy blessing, we beseech thee, to all parents and their children, that every Christian family may be an image of the Holy Family, and every Christian home a school of heavenly knowledge and virtuous living; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
