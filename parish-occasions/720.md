Give thy grace, O heavenly Father, to all Christian students in the universities and colleges of this land and overseas.  Deepen their devotion to thee and their commitment to thy service; and grant that both by the consistency of their lives and the faithfulness of their witness they may bring many to the knowledge of thee, and of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
