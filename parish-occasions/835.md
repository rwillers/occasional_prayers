Father, we thank thee for all the gains that life has brought.  At every stage of the journey thou hast been with us, and thy hand has guarded and guided our way.  Now, as life draws on towards its earthly close, let us not miss anything thou hast for us still, or fail to learn whatever thou art seeking to teach us.  O thou good and wise disposer, we are in thy hands, well contented so to be, and asking only that thy perfect will be fully done; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
