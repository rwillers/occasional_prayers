O Lord Jesus Christ, in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge: Have mercy on the students of this and every land who have set themselves to seek learning.  Guide them by thy Holy Spirit into all truth; grant them to realize that the fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; and so spread the knowledge of thy name throughout the student world that many may turn to thee and find thee as the Way, the Truth and the Life; for thine own name’s sake.
