source_order: 822

O Lord our Governor, who art King of all the earth: Accept of thine infinite goodness the offerings of thy people which, in obedience to thy commandment, in honour of thy name, and with a free will and joyful heart, we give and dedicate to thee; and grant that the same, being devoted to thy service, may be used for thy glory, and for the welfare of thy Church and people; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
