Heavenly Father, we thank thee for all thy love to us.  We remember again at this Christmas time the gift of the Lord Jesus and his birth at Bethlehem.  We pray that we may welcome him with gladness, and that there may always be room for him both in our hearts and in our homes.  We ask it in his name, who is our Saviour and our Friend.
