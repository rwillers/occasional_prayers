O Heavenly Father, we commend ourselves to thy grace and keeping.  Help us always to be of good courage, and let us never be disheartened by our difficulties or doubt thy love.  Give us grace to persevere patiently along life’s way, with a grateful recollection of thy past goodness, and a sure confidence in thy power to keep us to the end; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
