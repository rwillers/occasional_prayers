O God, strengthen and help those who are telling the good news of Jesus and working for thy kingdom overseas.  Help them to understand the people in the far countries where they work, and to love them and teach them wisely.  Comfort them when they are lonely, keep them well and strong, and grant that they may be full of the loving Spirit of Jesus.  For Jesus’ sake.
