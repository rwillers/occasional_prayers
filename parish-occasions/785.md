O Blessed Jesus, who from thy childhood didst make it thy whole employment to do thy Father’s will: Kindle in us a fervent zeal for thy glory, that we may consecrate our youth to thy service, and make it the great business of our lives to know and fear, to love and obey, our heavenly Father; who with thee and the Holy Spirit liveth and reigneth world without end.
