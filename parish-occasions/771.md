Merciful Father, regard with thy tender love and favour thy children.  Grant that as they have been admitted by baptism into the fellowship of thy people, so they may evermore grow in grace and in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.  Keep them in all temptation; teach them thy truth; and grant that they may early give their hearts to thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
