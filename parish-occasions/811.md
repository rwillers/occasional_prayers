title: 'Gift Day, or Stewardship Sunday — Generosity (4)'
layout: page
tags: [Various Occasions]
source_order: 811
---

Grant, O heavenly Father, that thy love may be so shed abroad in our hearts that we may count nothing too small to do for thee, nothing too much to give, and nothing too hard to endure; for his sake who laboured and strove and gave himself for us, thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
