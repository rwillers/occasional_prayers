Guide, O Lord, the minds of those who direct the work of education in this district, and prosper with thy blessing our schools and colleges; that our boys and girls and young people may be equipped with faith and knowledge for life’s journey, and go forth strong in spirit to serve their generation by thy will; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
