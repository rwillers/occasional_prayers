Eternal Father, whose gift is health and length of life: So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.  Grant that failing powers of body may be matched by increase of spiritual strength; so that trusting ourselves wholly to thy mercy and care, we may rest in thy will and patiently abide thy time; through thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
