Accept in thy mercy, O Lord God, the service of those who render thee praise in the choir of this church.  May they serve thee with glad hearts and dedicated lives; and grant that by the ministry of music thy name may be glorified and the hearts of thy people be uplifted in worship and love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
