Holy and eternal God, whose glory cherubim and seraphim and the hosts of heaven proclaim: Sanctify and bless, we beseech thee, the music of our worship and all who make it; and grant that the service that we can only offer unworthily here we may enjoy perfectly in thy heavenly kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
