Blessed be thou, O Lord God, for ever and ever.  Thine, O Lord, is the greatness, and the power, and the victory, and the majesty; for all that is in the heaven and in the earth is thine.  Thine is the kingdom, O Lord, and thou art exalted as head above all.  Both riches and honour come of thee, and of thine own do we now give thee, for the good of thy Church and the glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
