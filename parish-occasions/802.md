O God, who didst inspire the singers of Israel to glorify thee with thanksgiving, and to praise thee upon the strings and pipes: Accept our thanks for the beauty of craftsman’s art and music’s measure, and grant that we may so dedicate them to thy glory that thy worship may be duly adorned, and thy name worthily magnified; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
