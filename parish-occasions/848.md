O Lord our God, prosper, we pray thee, our efforts to fashion our citizen life here on earth after the pattern of the heavenly city, whose light is thy glory, and whose builder and maker thou art; through him whom thou hast sent to deliver us from all evil and to make all things new, Jesus Christ our Lord.
