We give thanks to thee, O God, for all the many blessings of this life; for health and strength and youth; for the joys of friendship and the opportunities of service; for the beauty of the world of nature; for the kindness and care of parents and for the blessings of home life.  Help us to repay, through those who need our help, some of the debt we owe.  We ask this for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
