O God, who in all ages hast called men to positions of leadership and responsibility in thy Church: Give thy grace, we humbly beseech thee, to all who have the duty of advancing the work of the Church in education, that they may serve before thee as faithful stewards in thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
