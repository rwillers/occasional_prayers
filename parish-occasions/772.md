O God, who hast commanded us to remember thee in the days of our youth, and dost promise that those who seek thee early shall find thee: Make our vision bright, our allegiance unfaltering, our service loyal; through him who as a boy was always about his Father’s business, Jesus Christ thy Son our Lord.
