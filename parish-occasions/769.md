O Heavenly Father, who long ago didst watch thy Son on earth grow as in stature so in wisdom and in perfect love of thee: Teach by the wondrous tale of Jesus and his Church the children whom thou watchest now, that they may grow into his likeness, loving thee, obedient to thy will, and happy in thy house; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
