Behold, O Lord our God, our strivings after a truer and more abiding order.  Scatter every excuse of frailty and unworthiness; consecrate us all with a heavenly mission; open to us a clearer prospect of our work; and give us strength according to our day gladly to welcome and fulfill it; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
