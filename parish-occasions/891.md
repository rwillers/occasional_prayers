O God of love, we pray for our hospitals, and for doctors and nurses in their work of mercy and healing.  Bless the sick, especially those known to us whom we remember in our hearts before thee. . . .  Do for them whatever is for their good, and comfort them with thy presence; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
