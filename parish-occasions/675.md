O Blessed Lord Jesus, who didst grow up in an earthly home obedient to thine earthly parents: Bless all the homes in this parish.  May the parents impart to their children the knowledge of thee and thy love, and may the children love, obey and succour their parents; and bring us all at last to the joy of thy heavenly home, for thy great name’s sake.
