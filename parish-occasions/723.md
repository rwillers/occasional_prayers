Almighty and everlasting God, we give thee humble thanks for the memory and good example of those who have laid down their lives in the service of our country.  We bless thee for their courage and devotion, even unto death.  Accept their sacrifice, we beseech thee; let it not be in vain that they have died in the cause of righteousness and honour; and in thy mercy send thy peace into the hearts of all men everywhere; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
