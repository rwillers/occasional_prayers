Great art thou, O Lord, and greatly to be praised; great is thy power, and thy wisdom is infinite.  Thee would we praise without ceasing.  Thou callest us to delight in thy praise, for thou hast made us for thyself, and our hearts find no rest until we rest in thee: to whom with the Father and the Holy Spirit all glory, praise and honour be ascribed, both now and for evermore.
