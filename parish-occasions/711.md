O Lord God Almighty, who through thy Son Jesus Christ didst reveal thine exceeding love for children: Graciously look upon those to whom thou hast entrusted the work of teaching.  Let them all be taught of thee; and so fill them with love to thee that through them thy love may be implanted in the hearts of those who are committed to their care; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
