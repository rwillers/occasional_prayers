O Lord Jesus Christ, who before thy passion didst join with thy disciples in a hymn of praise: Grant, we beseech thee, to those who offer the sacrifice of song in thy Church on earth that they may be admitted hereafter to have their part in the music of thy Church in heaven, and adore thee for ever; to whom with the Father and the Holy Spirit be all honour and glory, world without end.
