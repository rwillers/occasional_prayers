O Lord and heavenly Father, as on this day we bring our offerings to thee, we are mindful above all things of the manifold blessings which thou hast bestowed upon us, unworthy as we are of the least of them.  Thanks be to thee, O God, for every gift of thine, and most of all for the gift beyond words, thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord, to whom with thee and the Holy Spirit be glory now and for evermore.
