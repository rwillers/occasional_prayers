O Lord Jesus Christ, who wast found in the temple listening to the teachers and asking them questions: Be with the children who are taught in the Sunday schools of this parish, and grant them a right understanding of thy holy Word, and a readiness to do thy will; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
