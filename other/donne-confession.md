Forgive me, O Lord,<br>
O Lord, forgive me my sins,<br>
the sins of my youth,<br>
and my present sins,<br>
the sin that my parents cast upon me,<br>
original sin,<br>
and the sins that I cast upon my children,<br>
in an ill example;<br>
actual sins,<br>
sins which are manifest to all the world,<br>
and sins which I have so labored to hide from the world,<br>
as that now they are hid from mine own conscience,<br>
and mine own memory.

Forgive me my crying sins,<br>
and my whispering sins,<br>
sins of uncharitable hate,<br>
and sins of unchaste love,<br>
sins against thee and against thy power, O almighty Father,<br>
against thy wisdom, O glorious Son,<br>
against thy goodness, O blessed Spirit of God;<br>
and sins against him and him,<br>
against superiors and equals,<br>
and inferiors;<br>
and sins against me and me,<br>
against mine own soul,<br>
and against my body,<br>
which I have loved better than my soul.

Forgive me, O Lord,<br>
O Lord, in the merits of thy Christ and my Jesus,<br>
thine anointed,<br>
and my savior.

Forgive me my sins,<br>
all my sins,<br>
and I will put Christ to no more cost,<br>
nor thee to more trouble,<br>
for any reprobation or malediction that lay upon me,<br>
otherwise than as a sinner.

**Amen.**
