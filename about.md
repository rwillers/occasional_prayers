The *Book of Common Prayer*, which is less a book and more a series of texts, has rightly been recognized as a historic specimen of the English language. But it has also been and remains a work primarily intended for use in public and private worship. To that end, I offer this website as a collection of prayers “to be used for sundry Purposes” (1559).

My appreciation for these prayers, and frequent use of them, has been hampered in part by their lack of accessibility if one does not have a *Book of Common Prayer* (or several!) in hand. This website provides occasional prayers from several different editions of the *BCP*, in an indexed and searchable format.

I hope that you find these prayers helpful in worship and formative in belief, just as many generations of Anglicans have before us.

—R.D.W.
