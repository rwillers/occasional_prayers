O God, whose glory the countless hosts of heaven so ceaselessly proclaim: Graciously aid us, we beseech thee, as we seek worthily to fulfill our service and ministry in thy house; that in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs we may sing and make melody to thee with our whole heart; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
