O Almighty God, who hast called us to thy service in the fellowship of thy holy Church: Bless, we beseech thee, the Church of England Men’s Society in all its branches, both in this place and throughout the world.  Make all our members sound in faith and holy in life; bestow upon us the spirit of prayer and supplication; grant us by love to serve one another; and perfect in us that good work which thou hast begun; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
