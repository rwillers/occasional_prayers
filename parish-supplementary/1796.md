title: 'Various — Dedication of a House (1)'
layout: page

O Merciful Saviour, who with thy presence didst hallow an earthly home at Nazareth: Send thy blessing, we beseech thee, on all who dwell in this house; that, being preserved both in body and soul, they may offer to thee a faithful and willing service; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
