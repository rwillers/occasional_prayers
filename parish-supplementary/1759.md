O God, who hast given us minds to know thee, hearts to love thee, and voices to show forth thy praise: Give us grace, we beseech thee, to dedicate ourselves freely to thy service, that we may reverently fulfill the worship of thy sanctuary, and beautify the praises of thy house; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
