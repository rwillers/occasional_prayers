title: 'A Prayer Meeting — Concluding Prayer'
attribution: Frank Colquhoun

tags: [Supplementary Prayers]
source_order: 1730
---

Lord, it has been good for us to be here, for thou hast been here with us.  We thank thee for thy presence, and for the fellowship we have enjoyed one with another in this act of intercession.  And now, O Lord, we ask thee to accept our petitions, to answer them as seems best in thy sight, and to bless all those for whom we have prayed.  So send us now upon our way refreshed in spirit, and with thy peace in our hearts; and to thy name be praise and honour both now and for evermore.
