Almighty and everlasting God, from whom alone cometh wisdom and understanding: Be present, we pray thee, with thy servants here assembled; that seeking only thy honour and glory, we may wisely take counsel together for the good of thy Church and the advancement of thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
