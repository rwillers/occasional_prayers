O Lord and heavenly Father, who hast called us to serve thee in the administration of this school, and hast committed to us a solemn trust: Prosper, we beseech thee, the work of our hands and accept the service we now seek to render.  Give us understanding of thy will in all matters that lie before us, and also grace faithfully to discharge our duties; that in all things we may seek the true wellbeing of those who are in our care; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
