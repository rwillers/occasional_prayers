title: 'Offertory Prayers (3)'

tags: [Supplementary Prayers]
source_order: 1771

O God, the fountain of all good, we bring to thee our gifts, according as thou hast prospered us; and we pray thee to enable us, with our earthly things, to give thee the love of our hearts and the service of our lives; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
