This is the hour of prayer.  Draw near to us, O Lord, in the fullness of thy blessing as we wait upon thee.  May we know that thou art in our midst, according to thy word.  As thou hast given a place for prayer, so also give us hearts to pray; and grant that in the fellowship of intercession we may be drawn nearer to thee and to those for whom we pray; for thy honour and glory.
