O Lord, who hast taught us that the way of man is not in himself, and hast mercifully promised to keep the way of thy saints: Direct our thoughts and inspire our actions, we humbly pray thee, to the good of thy Church and the fulfillment of thy eternal purpose; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
