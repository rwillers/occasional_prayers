Almighty God, Creator and Sustainer of all things, who art pleased to receive the gifts thy children bring of thine own bounty: Regard us, we beseech thee, with thy favour who purpose to present this play in thy name; grant us the help of thy Holy Spirit, that we may be enabled to create the characters we are to play; and make us one body, working together in love to thy glory; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
