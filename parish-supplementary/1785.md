title: 'Various — Parish Visitors (2)'
layout: page
tags: ['Supplementary Prayers', 'Various']
source_order: 1785
---

Blessed Lord, who didst send thy disciples into every place whither thou thyself wouldest come: Pour out thy Holy Spirit upon these thy servants as they go forth in thy name, that they may humbly prepare the way before thee as messengers of thy peace; for the praise of thy holy name.
