O Lord and heavenly Father, who hast united us as members of one body in the work of thy Church in this parish: Give us grace faithfully and wisely to discharge our duties as in thy sight.  Guide us by thy Holy Spirit in all our deliberations, and bring our minds into accordance with thy perfect will.  Keep our vision clear, our sympathies broad, our resolution firm, our zeal unabating; and grant that whatever we do, in word or deed, we may do all for thy glory, and in the name of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
