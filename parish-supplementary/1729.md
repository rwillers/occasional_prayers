O Almighty God, we believe that thou art here present; help us to remember thy presence.  Thou knowest all things; there is nothing in us but thou, O Lord, knowest it altogether.  Help us in the prayers we are about to offer for thy Church, for the world, and for ourselves, that what we ask may be according to thy will and for the enlargement of thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
