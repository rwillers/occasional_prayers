O Eternal God, the fountain of all wisdom, who didst send thy Holy Spirit to lead thy disciples into all truth: Vouchsafe his gracious presence, we beseech thee, to thy servants here assembled; and grant that he may so rule our hearts and guide our counsels that in all things we may seek only thy glory and the good of thy holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
