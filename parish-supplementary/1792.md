Before our play begins we offer it to thee, O Lord, beseeching thee to accept our good intention, for our desire is to do thee service.  Assist our art, we pray thee, that by thy grace we may offer our best to thee; and grant that in fellowship with our audience we may rejoice in thy goodness and make manifest thy praise; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
