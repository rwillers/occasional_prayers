---
title: 'Various — Laying of a Foundation Stone (2)'
layout: page
tags: ['Supplementary Prayers', 'Various']
source_order: 1795
---

O Lord Jesus Christ, who art the one foundation of thy Church and its chief cornerstone: Bless the laying of this stone in thy name, we humbly beseech thee; and be thou the beginning, the increase, and the consummation of this work which is undertaken to thy glory; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
