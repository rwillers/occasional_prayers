O Lord Christ, who on the night before thy passion didst have a last supper with thine apostles and prayed that they might be one family, united in loyalty to thee and in the service of mankind: Inspire, we pray thee, our fellowship with the spirit of love and sacrifice, that we may be true to thee and to one another, and serve thee gladly in thy Church all the days of our life; to the glory of God the Father.
