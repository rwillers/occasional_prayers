O Holy and eternal God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, whom all the companies of heaven laud and adore: Graciously vouchsafe to receive the offering we are about to make; and so bless us, as we magnify thee upon earth with music and the voice of melody, that hereafter we may sing the new song in the heavenly city; where thou reignest, almighty, all glorious, world without end.
