---
title: For Those Who Work on Land or Sea (Rogation)
layout: page
tags: ['Rogation Days']
source_order: 14
---

Almighty God, whose will it is that the earth and the sea should bear fruit in due season: bless the labours of those who work on land and sea, grant us a good harvest and the grace always to rejoice in your fatherly care; through Jesus Christ your Son our Lord.
