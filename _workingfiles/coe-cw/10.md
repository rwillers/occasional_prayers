title: For the Guidance of the Holy Spirit at a Synod or Parochial Church Council Meeting

---

Almighty God, you have given your Holy Spirit to the Church to lead us into all truth: bless with the Spirit’s grace and presence the members of this synod/PCC/etc.; keep us/them steadfast in faith and united in love, that we/they may manifest your glory and prepare the way of your kingdom; through Jesus Christ your Son our Lord.
