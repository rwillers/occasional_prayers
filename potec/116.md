---
title: For Love of God’s Law
layout: page
tags: ['Fifth Century Prayers', 'Leonine Sacramentary']
source_order: 116
---

We beseech Thee, O Lord, be gracious to Thy people, that we, leaving day by day the things which displease Thee, may be more and more filled with the love of Thy commandments, and being supported by Thy comfort in this present life, may advance to the full enjoyment of life immortal; through Jesus Christ our Lord. **Amen.**
