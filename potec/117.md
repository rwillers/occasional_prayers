---
title: For Christ’s Presence
layout: page
tags: ['Fifth Century Prayers', 'Leonine Sacramentary']
source_order: 117
---

Be present, O Lord, to our supplications; that as we trust that the Saviour of mankind is seated with Thee in Thy Majesty, so we may feel that, according to His promise, He abideth with us unto the end of the world; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord. **Amen.**
