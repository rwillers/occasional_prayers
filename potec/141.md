---
title: For Forgiveness
layout: page
tags: ['Fifth Century Prayers', 'Gelasian Sacramentary']
source_order: 141
---

We beseech Thee, Almighty God, to receive with Fatherly tenderness Thy people fleeing from Thine anger to Thyself; that they who dread the scourge that comes from Thy Majesty may be enabled to rejoice in Thy forgiveness; through Jesus Christ our Lord. **Amen.**
