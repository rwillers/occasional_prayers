title: An Evening Thanksgiving

tags: ['Fifth Century Prayers', 'Thanksgivings']

Accept, we beseech Thee, our evening thanksgiving, O Thou Fountain of all good, who hast led us in safety through the length of the day; Who daily blessest us with so many temporal mercies, and hast given us the hope of resurrection to eternal life; through Jesus Christ our Lord. **Amen.**
