---
title: For Grace and Protection
layout: page
tags: ['Fifth Century Prayers']
source_order: 125
---

Incline mercifully Thine ear, O Lord, to these our prayers, and fill our hearts with Thy grace, that loving Thee with an unfeigned love we may evermore be defended under Thy most gracious protection, and be accepted in all our prayers and services; through Jesus Christ our Lord. **Amen.**
