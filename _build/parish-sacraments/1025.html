Prayers Which May Be Used at the Burial of One Who Has Taken His Own Life — The Burial of a Child (2)

Convocation of York

Holy and loving Father, who graciously showest mercy to thy children though they rebel against thee: Remember thy servant N. according to the favour that thou bearest unto thy people, and grant unto him forgiveness of all his sins and a place in the kingdom of thy Son, our Saviour Jesus Christ; who liveth and reigneth with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
