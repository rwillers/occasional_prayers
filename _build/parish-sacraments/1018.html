The Departed — Those Who Mourn (3)

Church of South India

Father of mercies and God of all comfort, look in thy tender love and pity, we beseech thee, on thy sorrowing servants. Be thou to them their refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble; make them to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge; who by death hath conquered death, and by rising again hath opened the gates of everlasting life, even Jesus Christ our Lord.
