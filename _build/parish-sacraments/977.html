Childbirth — After Childbirth (3)

Guild of Health

Almighty God, we give thee hearty thanks for thy loving care during this woman’s pregnancy and delivery, and for the skill and devotion of all who have guarded the health of both mother and child. Grant that she, remembering thy love for her, may serve thee faithfully in the daily care of her home and family; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
