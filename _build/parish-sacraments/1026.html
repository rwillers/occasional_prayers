Prayers Which May Be Used at the Burial of One Who Has Taken His Own Life (3)

Book of Common Order

Father of mercies and God of all comfort, who madest nothing in vain, and lovest all that thou hast made: Look in tender pity upon thy bereaved servants, that they may be enabled to find in thee their refuge and their strength, and may be delivered out of their distresses; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
