The Church Overseas — Followers of the Religions of the East

O God, who wiliest that all men should come to the knowledge of the truth, and hast not left thyself without witness anywhere in the world: We pray for Hindus, Buddhists and all others who are seeking after thee through the good that is known to them. We beseech thee to illuminate their hearts and minds with the knowledge of thy glory in the face of Jesus Christ, that henceforth they may walk in thy light and in thy love; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
