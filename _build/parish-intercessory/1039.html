The Coming of God’s Kingdom (6)

In Watchings Often

O Thou, whose kingdom is without territory yet covers all lands, whose realm is without boundaries yet crosses all frontiers: Extend thine empire over human hearts, and break down all adverse barriers which keep mankind asunder. Grant to the nations of the world the saving knowledge of thy love, so that there may be one kingdom of righteousness and truth in which all peoples may praise thy glorious name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
