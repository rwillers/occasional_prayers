Education and Youth — Any Youth Organization

Help us, O Lord, who are united in the fellowship of Scouts [or Guides, the Boys’ Brigade, etc.] throughout the world, to be worthy of our high calling. Give us grace to keep our vows and to fight manfully under thy banner, that in the end we may be found true citizens of thy heavenly kingdom; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
