The Coming of God’s Kingdom — A Bidding Prayer (7)

R. Ambrose Reeves

O Eternal God, the Father of all mankind, in whom we live and move and have our being: Have mercy on the whole human race. Pity their ignorance, their foolishness, their weakness, their sin. Set up an ensign for the nations, O Lord; hasten thy kingdom; and bring in everlasting righteousness; for the honour of thy Son, our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
