In Time of War — For a Righteous Peace

Form of National Prayer, May 1940

O Almighty God, who canst bring good out of evil, and makest even the wrath of man turn to thy praise: We beseech thee so to order and dispose the issue of this war that we may be brought through strife to lasting peace; and that the nations of the world may be united in a new fellowship for the promotion of thy glory and the good of all mankind; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
