In Time of War — In Time of War (1)

National Mission, 1916

Lord God Almighty, who hast brought thy judgments upon all the earth, that the inhabitants of the world may learn righteousness: We entreat thy divine Majesty so to turn the hearts of the people of this land, that sorrowing for our sins with true repentance, and trusting in the hope of thy salvation, we may be renewed to do thee service and show forth thy praise from one generation to another; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
