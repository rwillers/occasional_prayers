  3. For an Anniversary of One Departed

Almighty God, we remember this day before you your faithful servant N., and we pray that, having opened to him the gates of larger life, you will receive him more and more into your joyful service, that he may win, with you and your servants everywhere, the eternal victory; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
