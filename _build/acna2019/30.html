For Civil Authorities

Almighty God, our heavenly Father, send down on those who hold public office [especially ___] the spirit of wisdom, charity, and justice; that with steadfast purpose they may faithfully serve in their offices to promote the well being of all people; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.
