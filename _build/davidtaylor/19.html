A Prayer for Stir-Crazy Children

Dear Jesus, I feel frustrated right now. I don’t know what to do with myself and I need your help. I feel tired and bored and my feelings are all mixed up and out of place. But you can help me, and I thank you that you know exactly how I feel and that you have a special place in your heart for children like me—and that you love me very much. Please give me that peace that you told your disciples you have lots off. I need it. Thank you. In your name I pray. Amen. PS: Please help mommy and daddy too.
