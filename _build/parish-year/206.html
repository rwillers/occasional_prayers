Holy Week — Palm Sunday — The Blessing of Palm (1)

Diocese of Southwark

Almighty God, who gavest thine only Son to suffer upon earth for our redemption: Send thy blessing upon us who now make our prayers unto thee, and sanctify to our use these branches of palm; that all who shall take them in thy name may enjoy the freedom of thy kingdom, and serve thee in all good works; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
