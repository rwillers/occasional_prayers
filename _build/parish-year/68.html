Christmas — Christmas Day — Christmas Morning

Luke 2:15; Isaiah 9:6

Let us now go even unto Bethlehem and see this thing which is come to pass, which the Lord hath made known unto us. For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given, and the government shall be upon his shoulder; and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The Mighty God, The Everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.
