In the Ember Weeks — For the Increase of the Ministry (4)

O Lord of the harvest, raise up, we pray thee, faithful and true men for the work of thy Church. Equip them for thy service; enrich them with thy grace; and send them forth in due time to gather fruit unto eternal life; for the glory of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
