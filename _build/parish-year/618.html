In the Ember Weeks — For the Increase of the Ministry (8)

South African Prayer Book

We beseech thee, Almighty God, to call many to the ministry of thy Church; and to those whom thou dost call give thy grace that they may hear and answer thy call; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
