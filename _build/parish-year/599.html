Lesser Feasts — St. George’s Day (April 23) (2)

O Lord our God, who didst endue thy blessed martyr, Saint George, with grace to act and endure valiantly, so that he has become to our people an example of heroic strength: Purge us from all falsity and other weakness, we humbly beseech thee, that we too may be strong in the power of thy might; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
