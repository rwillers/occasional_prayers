Eastertide — Second Sunday After Easter — The Good Shepherd (2)

O Lord Jesus Christ, thou good shepherd of the sheep, who camest to seek the lost and to gather them into thy fold: Have compassion on those who have wandered from thee; feed those who hunger, cause the weary to lie down in thy pastures, bind up those who are broken in heart, and strengthen those who are weak; and lead us all, O Lord, in the paths of righteousness, for thy name’s sake.
