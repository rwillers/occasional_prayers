Septuagesima to Quinquagesima — Sunday Called Septuagesima — Maker of Heaven and Earth (1)

Revelation 4:11; 5:13

Worthy art thou, our Lord and our God, to receive glory and honour and power, for thou didst create all things, and by thy will they existed and were created.

To him who sits upon the throne and to the Lamb be blessing and honour and glory and might for ever and ever!
