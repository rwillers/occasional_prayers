Pulpit Prayers (4)

Based on 2 Corinthians 4:6

O God, who didst command the light to shine out of darkness: We pray thee to shine into our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of thy glory in the face of Jesus Christ; to whom be praise and honour for evermore.
