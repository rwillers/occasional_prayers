At Evening (13)

Lutheran Church

Abide with us, Lord, for it is toward evening and the day is far spent. Abide with us and with thy whole Church. Abide with us in the end of the day, in the end of our life, in the end of the world. Abide with us in thy grace and bounty, with thy holy Word and sacrament, with thy comfort and blessing. Abide with us when cometh the night of affliction and fear, the night of doubt and temptation, the night of bitter death. Abide with us and with all thy faithful ones, O Lord, in time and in eternity.
