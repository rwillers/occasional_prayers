Benedictions and Doxologies — Benedictions (4)

William Temple

May the love of the Lord Jesus draw us to himself; may the power of the Lord Jesus strengthen us in his service; may the joy of the Lord Jesus fill our souls; and may the blessing of God Almighty, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, be with you and abide with you always.
