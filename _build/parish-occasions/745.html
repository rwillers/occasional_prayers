Dedication Festival — For a Parish Church (1)

O Almighty Father, who art adored by the holy angels, and yet art pleased to accept the praises of sinful men: Let thy glory fill this house of prayer, we beseech thee; and mercifully grant that all who worship thee here may be numbered at the last with those who sing the new song before thy heavenly throne; through Jesus Christ our Lord, who livest and reignest with thee and the Holy Ghost, ever one God, world without end.
