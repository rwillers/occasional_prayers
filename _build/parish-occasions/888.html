Children’s Church — Missionaries (2)

Canadian Prayer Book

Heavenly Father, we thank thee for loving us, and for sending thy dear Son into the world to save us from our sins. We pray to thee for all who do not know of thy love, both at home and in other lands. Grant that through the work of our missionaries they may be led to confess the faith of Christ crucified, and with us become soldiers and servants of thy holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
