Mothering Sunday Service — For Our Homes and Family Life (4)

From the Order of Compline

Visit, we beseech thee, O Lord, our homes, and drive from them all the snares of the evil one; let thy holy angels dwell in them to preserve all who live there in peace; and may thy blessing be upon us evermore; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
