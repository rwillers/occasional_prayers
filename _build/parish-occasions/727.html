Remembrance Sunday — For the Peace of the World (3)

Suzanne de Dietrich

O Lord our God, who hast reconciled us to thyself and to one another through the death of thy Son, and hast entrusted to us the ministry of reconciliation: Keep ever before our hearts and minds the price that thou hast paid for the salvation of the world, and let the cross of thy Son bear in us and in all men its fruits of righteousness and peace; for his name’s sake.
