Remembrance Sunday — For the Peace of the World (2)

O God, whose righteous wisdom governs the heavens, and controls also the destinies of men: Teach the rulers of the nations the things that belong to their peace. Open the eyes of all men to see the sovereignty of thine eternal truth and love; that being delivered from the bondage of human vanity and corruption, they may find their freedom in obedience to thy laws and their brotherhood in the unity of thy service; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
