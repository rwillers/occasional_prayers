Education Sunday — For Christian Education (4)

Prayers for Education Sunday, 1957

O Lord, who didst found thy Church to proclaim the Faith by preaching and teaching: Give to our leaders in religious education undaunted courage and wisdom, and to us a new spirit of faith and adventure, so that thy will may be done on earth as it is in heaven, to the glory of thy name.
