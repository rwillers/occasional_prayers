Old People’s Service — For the Aged (1)

George Appleton

O Lord Jesus Christ, who didst hear the prayer of thy two disciples and didst abide with them at eventide: Abide, we pray thee, with all thy people in the evening of life. Make thyself known to them, and let thy light shine upon their path; and whenever they shall pass through the valley of the shadow of death, be with them unto the end; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
