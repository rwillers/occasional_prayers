A Memorial Service — In Memoriam (4)

We give thee thanks, O Lord Christ, that the life which we now live in thee is part of the life which is eternal, and the fellowship which we have with thee unites us with our brethren both on earth and in heaven. Grant that as we journey through the years we may know joys which are without end, and at the last come to that abiding city where thou livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
