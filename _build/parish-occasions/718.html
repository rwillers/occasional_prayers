Education Sunday — For the National Society

Bless, we beseech thee, Almighty God, the work of the National Society on behalf of religious education, and grant that thy Holy Spirit may direct and rule the hearts of all who labour for the advancement of true religion. Give to them that teach the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and to those who learn grace to love the truth, that we may ever be a righteous and God-fearing people; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
