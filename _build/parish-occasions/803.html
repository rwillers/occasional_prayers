Choir Festival — Concluding Prayers (1)

Harold Anson

O God, who in the gift of music hath given us a revelation of thy divine beauty: Teach us to love thee in all thy gifts, and so to devote ourselves in all our work to thy glory, that through music we may raise men from the sorrows of this world to the enjoyment of thy divine loveliness; through thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
