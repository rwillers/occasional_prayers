O God, who hast taught us that love is the fulfilling of the law: Help us by thy Holy Spirit so to love thee that we may evermore seek to do thy holy will; and so to love our neighbour that we may in all things do to others as we would that they should do unto us; for the sake of him who loved us and gave himself for us, thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
