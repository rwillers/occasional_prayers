title: 'The Christian Warfare (6)'
attribution: Frank Colquhoun
layout: page
tags: [Devotional Prayers]
source_order: 1647
---

O Lord God, who hast called us to be thy soldiers and to fight manfully under Christ’s banner: Enable us constantly to contend for truth, goodness, and justice without fear or favour; and relentlessly to oppose evil wherever it is found, and whatever form it takes; in the name and strength of Jesus Christ our Saviour.
