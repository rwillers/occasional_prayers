title: 'Various — For Sincerity (3)'

tags: [Devotional Prayers]

Uphold us, O Lord of life, with thy free Spirit.  Save us from unreality, from praising what is lofty while practising what is base, from thinking high thoughts while living a poor life.  Keep us from ignoble fears, and send us on our way with hope; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
