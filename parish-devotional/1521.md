O Lord God, most high, most holy, to whom angels and archangels and all the host of heaven ascribe unceasing praise: Accept the homage and adoration of us thy humble servants who are not worthy to draw near to thee but for the infinite merits of thy Son, our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
