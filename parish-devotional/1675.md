My God, I believe in thee, and all that thy Church doth teach, because thou hast said it and thy Word is true.<br>My God, I hope in thee, for grace and for glory, because of thy promises, thy mercy, and thy power.<br>My God, I love thee, because thou art good; and for thy sake I love my neighbour as myself.
