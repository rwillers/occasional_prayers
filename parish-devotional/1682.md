Grant us, O Lord our God, ever to find in thee a very present help in trouble.<br>When we are in the darkness of doubt or perplexity, shed thy light upon our way.<br>When we are burdened with the affairs of our daily life, lift us to the calm of thy presence.<br>When we are battling with temptation and the flesh is weak, by the might of thy Spirit make us strong to overcome.<br>We ask these things through him in whom we are more than conquerors, thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
