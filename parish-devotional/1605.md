O God our Father, who in thy great wisdom hast prepared for each one of us our task to do for thee: Grant that we may seek to know thy will, and may dedicate ourselves to serve thee wherever we are needed.  May we never from love of ease or from any fear turn from the path which thou dost appoint, but steafastly walk in thy way, through the grace of thy Holy Spirit; for the glory of Jesus Christ our Lord.
