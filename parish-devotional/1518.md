Blessing and honour, and thanksgiving and praise,<br>more than we can utter,<br>more than we can conceive,<br>be unto thee, O holy and glorious Trinity,<br>Father, Son, and Holy Spirit,<br>by all angels, all men, all creatures,<br>for ever and ever.
