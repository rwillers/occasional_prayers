We thank thee, Lord, for all thy goodness to us:<br>for the world which thou hast made, and for strength to serve thee in it;<br>for daily revelations of thyself, in nature, in art, and in the lives of men;<br>for the Bible, through which thy Word is spoken to men;<br>for all other writings which reveal thy truth;<br>for the tasks to which thou art calling us.<br>But above all we thank thee for thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ, through whom we see thee, who art our Father.<br>May his Spirit dwell within us, making us to serve thee with gladness all the days of our life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
