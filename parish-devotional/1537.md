Merciful God, to thee we commend ourselves and all those who need thy help and correction.<br>Where there is hatred, give love;<br>Where there is injury, pardon;<br>Where there is doubt, faith;<br>Where there is despair, hope;<br>Where there is sadness, joy;<br>Where there is darkness, light.<br>Grant that we may not seek so much to be consoled, as to console;<br>to be understood, as to understand;<br>to be loved, as to love;<br>For in giving we receive, in pardoning we are pardoned, and dying we are born into eternal life.
