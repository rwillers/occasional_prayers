May the strength of God pilot us.<br>May the power of God preserve us.<br>May the wisdom of God instruct us.<br>May the hand of God protect us.<br>May the way of God direct us.<br>May the shield of God defend us.<br>May the host of God guard us against the snares of evil<br>and the temptations of the world.<br>May Christ be with us, Christ before us, Christ in us, Christ over us.<br>May thy salvation, O Lord, be always ours this day and for evermore.
