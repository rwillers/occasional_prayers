Lord Jesus, my Saviour, let me now come to thee:<br>My heart is cold; O Lord, warm it with thy selfless love.<br>My heart is sinful; cleanse it by thy precious blood.<br>My heart is weak; strengthen it by thy joyous Spirit.<br>My heart is empty; fill it with thy divine presence.<br>Lord Jesus, my heart is thine; possess it always and only for thyself.
