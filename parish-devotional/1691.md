May the Lord of his great mercy bless you, and give you understanding of his wisdom and grace;<br>May he nourish you with the riches of the Catholic Faith, and make you to persevere in all good works;<br>May he keep your steps from wandering, and direct you into the paths of love and peace;<br>And the blessing of God Almighty, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, be upon you and remain with you always.
