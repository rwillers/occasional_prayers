Into thy hands, O Lord, we commend ourselves and all whom we love, beseeching thee to keep us under the shadow of thy wings.  Drive from us every evil thing; give thine angels ,charge over us; guard us ever, in life or death, that whether we wake or sleep, we may live together with thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
