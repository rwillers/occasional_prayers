Heavenly Father, who hast taught us to be anxious for nothing, but to cast all our care upon thee: Increase our faith, we beseech thee, and strengthen our resolve; that with a calm and courageous spirit we may meet all life’s duties and demands, and know in daily experience thy peace which passes all understanding; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
