attribution: St. Augustine

tags: ['Devotional Prayers', 'At Evening', 'St. Augustine']
source_order: 1512

Watch thou, dear Lord, with those who wake, or watch, or weep tonight, and give thine angels charge over those who sleep.  Tend thy sick ones, O Lord Christ.  Rest thy weary ones.  Bless thy dying ones.  Soothe thy suffering ones.  Pity thine afflicted ones.  And all for thy love’s sake.
