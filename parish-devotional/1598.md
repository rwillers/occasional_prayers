We thank thee, O God, that thou hast given us powers of mind to search into thy laws and to understand thy truth; and we pray that our reverence may increase with our knowledge, so that we may be led to worship thee in deepening humility, and may devote ourselves to the fulfillment of thy great purposes for mankind; for the glory of Jesus Christ our Lord.
