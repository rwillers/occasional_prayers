Bless us, O God the Father, who hast created us.<br>Bless us, O God the Son, who hast redeemed us.<br>Bless us, O God the Holy Spirit, who sanctifieth us.<br>O Blessed Trinity, keep us in body, soul, and spirit unto everlasting life.
