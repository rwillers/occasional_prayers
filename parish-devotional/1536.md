Eternal Light, shine into our hearts;<br>Eternal Goodness, deliver us from evil;<br>Eternal Power, be our support;<br>Eternal Wisdom, scatter the darkness of our ignorance;<br>Eternal Pity, have mercy upon us;<br>that with all our heart and mind and soul and strength we may seek thy face and be brought by thine infinite mercy to thy holy presence; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
