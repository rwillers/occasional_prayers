title: 'At Evening (11)'

tags: [Devotional Prayers, At Times of Prayer and Worship]

O Lord Jesus Christ, to whom the sick were brought that they might be healed, and who didst send none of them away without thy blessing: Look in thy pity on all who come to thee this night for healing of body or mind or spirit, and grant them thy comfort and help; for thy tender mercies’ sake.
