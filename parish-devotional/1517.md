Worthy of praise from every mouth, of confession from every tongue, of worship from every creature, is thy glorious name, O Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, who didst create the world by the word of thy power, and in love didst wonderfully redeem it.  Wherefore with angels, and archangels, and all the company of heaven, we adore and magnify thy holy name, evermore praising thee, and saying:<br>Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of Hosts,<br>Heaven and earth are full of thy glory:<br>Glory to be thee, O Lord, most High.
