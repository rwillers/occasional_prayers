---
title: Prayer for Parents Whose Children Have Abandoned the Faith
layout: page
tags: ['Those in Need', 'W. David O. Taylor']
source_order: 7
---

O Lord, you whose heart is broken for those who have journeyed into the far country, I pray that you would give me the grace to bear the sorrow of my own child who finds <em>himself</em> in the far country: hope in the face of despair, patience for a heart that wants to give up, and the ability to remain present even when it feels painful, so that, like the prodigal son's father, I too might keep perpetual watch and retain a heart of compassion for my child whom you, O Christ, love with a fierce and indefatigable love. In your holy name. **Amen.**
