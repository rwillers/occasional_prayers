---
title: A Prayer for a New Morning
layout: page
tags: ['Those in Need', 'W. David O. Taylor']
source_order: 24
---

Oh Lord, may I walk in the way of life today, faithful to what you have entrusted to me for this day, no more and no less. Let me not become anxious over the things that I cannot control but instead receive your grace to face these things trustingly. In Jesus’ name. **Amen.**
