---
title: A Prayer for Single People
layout: page
tags: [Those in Need]
source_order: 16
---

Oh Lord, you who were alone in the wilderness and in the garden, be with me in this time of singleness and solitude; and when I feel tempted to believe that I have been abandoned by friends and forsaken by God, may I sense the sweet presence of your Spirit and the ministering care of your angels, nourishing me in my hour of need, so that I might know that I am never alone but rather filled and fortified by your very real presence. In your name. **Amen.**
