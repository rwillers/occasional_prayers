---
title: A Prayer for Health Workers
layout: page
tags: ['Those in Need', 'W. David O. Taylor']
source_order: 13
---

Oh Lord, you who touched the leper and healed the sick, Be with me this day. Be a succor in the time of trial. Be a comfort in the hour of loss. Be a refuge in a storm of needs. Use my hands to heal the broken in body And my words to heal the crushed in spirit. Make things possible, I pray, in the face of all that seems impossible. Be my haven, dear Jesus, and my true companion Throughout these tireless days and lonely nights. In your compassionate name. **Amen.**
