---
title: 'Travel — Seafarers (1)'
attribution: Scottish Prayer Book
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1240
---

O Almighty God, whose way is in the sea and whose paths are in the great waters: Be present, we beseech thee, with our brethren in the manifold dangers of the deep; protect them from all perils, prosper them in their course, and bring them in safety to the haven where they would be, with a grateful sense of thy mercies; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
