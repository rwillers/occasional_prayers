---
title: 'Society and Industry — Those Who Work by Night'
attribution: New Every Morning
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1160
---

Bless, O Lord, all those who, in the night, watch over our lives and homes, and guard all who through the hours of darkness carry on the unresting commerce of men by land and sea and air.  Grant them rest and refreshment, and make us thankful for their service; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
