title: 'The Suffering — The Sick in Body (6)'

O Lord Jesus Christ, unto whom the sick were brought that they might be healed, and thou didst send none of them away without thy blessing: Look in pity upon all who come to thee for healing of body or mind, and grant them the blessing of thy saving health, both now and evermore.
