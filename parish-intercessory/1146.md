---
title: 'Society and Industry — For a Blessing on Local Industries'
attribution: Irish Prayer Book
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1146
---

O Almighty Father, who through thy Son Jesus Christ hast consecrated labour to the blessing of mankind: Prosper, we pray thee, the industries of this place; defend those who are engaged therein from all perils, and grant that they may rejoice in the fruits of thy bounty, and bless thee for thy loving-kindness; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
