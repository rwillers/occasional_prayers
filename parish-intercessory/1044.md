---
title: 'The Coming of God’s Kingdom (11)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1044
---

Enlarge thy kingdom, O God, and deliver the world from the tyranny of Satan.  Hasten the time, which thy Spirit hath foretold, when all nations whom thou hast made shall worship thee and glorify thy name.  Bless the endeavours of those who strive to propagate thy holy gospel, and prepare the hearts of all men to receive it; to the honour and glory of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
