O Blessed Saviour, who wart pleased thyself to be numbered among the craftsmen: We pray thee to guide and prosper all who labour with mind and hand, that their work may be done for thy honour, and rewarded with thy approval; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
