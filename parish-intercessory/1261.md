---
title: 'The Suffering — Those in Need (12)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1261
---

O Heavenly Father, look in thy compassion on those in need, especially on those for whom our prayers are desired.  Grant them the comfort of thy presence; let thy peace rule in their hearts; and fulfill in them thy perfect will, for the glory of Jesus Christ our Saviour.
