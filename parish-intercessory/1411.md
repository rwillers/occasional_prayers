O God, our heavenly Father, we humbly pray thee to bless abundantly the efforts that are now being made (or, about to be made) to turn the people of this parish to thyself in sincere repentance and living faith.  Prepare all hearts to receive the seed of thy Word, and grant that it may take deep root and bring forth fruit to thy glory; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
