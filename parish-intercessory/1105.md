O God our Father, who through thy Son Jesus Christ hast commanded us to preach the gospel to every creature: Mercifully grant unto all those who broadcast thy Word the light of thy Holy Spirit, and to those who listen the gift of understanding; so that thy praise may be in all the earth, who livest and reignest one God for evermore.
