O Lord, our heavenly Father, almighty, everlasting God, who givest light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death: Lift up, we beseech thee, the light of thy countenance upon those who are wandering uncertain and doubtful in the night of this world.  Make known to them the way of truth and peace, and open the eyes of their understanding to acknowledge thee, the living God, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; to whom be praise and dominion for ever and ever.
