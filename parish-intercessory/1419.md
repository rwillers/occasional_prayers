O Lord Jesus Christ, who hast called those who believe on thee to be evangelists of thy kingdom and to carry thy gospel to the souls of men: Renew in us the spirit of faith and service; enlighten our minds to understand thy message; and inflame our hearts with such love toward thee that thy word upon our lips may be mighty to overthrow the powers of darkness, and to turn the feet of many into thy way of light and peace; to the glory of thy name.
