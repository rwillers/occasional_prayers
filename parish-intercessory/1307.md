Almighty and everlasting God, who hast revealed thy glory in Christ to all nations: Protect, we beseech thee, what thy compassion has created, that thy Church which is spread abroad throughout the world may persevere with steadfast faith in the confession of thy name; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
