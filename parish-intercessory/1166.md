---
title: 'Education and Youth — Those Leaving School'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1166
---

We commend to thy fatherly care, O Lord, those who are about to leave this school.  May thy goodness and mercy follow them throughout their life.  Guard them in danger and keep them from evil; guide them continually in the right way; and assist them in every good work, for the glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
