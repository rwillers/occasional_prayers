O God of unchangeable power and eternal light, look favourably upon thy whole Church and make it the instrument of thy saving purpose for the world; that the life of mankind, redeemed in Christ, may everywhere be renewed, and all things may return into unity through him from whom they took their origin, even the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
