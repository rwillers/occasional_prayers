O thou true Light that lightest every man, coming into the world: Do thou in thy mercy touch the hearts and enlighten the understanding of all whom thou dost call to the service of thy Church; that they may cheerfully acknowledge and readily obey thy claims upon their lives, to the benefit of thy people and the glory of thy holy name, who with the Father and the Holy Spirit livest and reignest, world without end.
