O Lord God, who wiliest that all men should be saved and come to the knowledge of thy Son, our Saviour: Give thy Holy Spirit to all in thy Church in the diocese of ——; that by the setting forth of Christ as Saviour many may find new life, and thy Church be extended and united in her true work of bringing men to thee; in the name and power of Jesus Christ our Lord.
