Heavenly Father, God both of righteousness and peace: Have mercy upon the nations now engaged in bitter war.  Cleanse both us and our enemies of hatred and covetousness; and make us so worthy of our cause, so steadfast in thy strength, that no weakness may delay the victory of our arms, and no selfishness mar the righteousness of our peace; for Jesus Christ’s sake.
