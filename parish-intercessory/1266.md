O God, whose compassions fail not, whose faithfulness is new every morning: Draw near, we pray thee, to those who are stricken with sickness and pain.  Ease their discomfort, allay their fears strengthen their faith, be thou their salvation and their peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
