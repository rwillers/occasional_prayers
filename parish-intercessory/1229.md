---
title: 'Home and Friends — Those We Love (3)'
attribution: Gregorian Sacramentary
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1229
---

O God, who by the grace of thy Holy Spirit hast shed thy love abroad in the hearts of thy faithful people: Mercifully grant to those whom we love health of body and soul; that they may serve thee with all their strength and gladly fulfill all thy good pleasure; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
