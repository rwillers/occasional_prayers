O Lord God Almighty, who hast made for thy glory all nations over the face of the earth, that they may do thee service in the joy of freedom; give to this people of England the passion of righteousness, and the strength of self-control, that they may exercise their liberty with a serious and single desire to fulfill thy gracious will; through Jesus Christ, our Master, Redeemer, and King.
