O King of kings, help us to become worthy citizens of our country and commonwealth.  Give us such a sense of responsibility that, shunning all false pride and narrow prejudices, we may use the privileges we have inherited as a means of promoting justice and peace, health and happiness, among the nations of the earth, and may work for the unity of all men in the citizenship of thy heavenly kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
