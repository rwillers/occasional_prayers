O Lord, thou God of righteousness and truth, grant to our Queen and her government, to members of parliament and all in positions of responsibility, the guidance of thy Spirit.  May they never lead the nation wrongly through love of power, desire to please, or unworthy ideals, but always love righteousness and truth; so may thy kingdom be advanced and thy name be hallowed; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
