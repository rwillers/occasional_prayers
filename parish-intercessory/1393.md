O God, who hast graciously united us in a goodly fellowship: We pray for thy Church in this diocese and for thy people in our parishes, that comeliness of worship, preaching of the faith, and holiness of living may always abound and everywhere be spread abroad among us; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
