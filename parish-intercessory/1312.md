Most gracious Father, we most humbly beseech thee for thy holy catholic Church.  Fill it with all truth; in all truth with all peace.  Where it is corrupt, purge it; where it is in error, direct it; where anything is amiss, reform it; where it is right, strengthen and confirm it; where it is in want, furnish it; where it is divided, heal it, and unite it in thy love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
