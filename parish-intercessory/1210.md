O Merciful Father, look with thy tender compassion upon all prisoners of war.  Supply all their needs, and hasten the time of their release; let thy love protect them and thy presence cheer them, that day by day in weariness and hardship they may have strength to endure patiently, and may find peace in thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
