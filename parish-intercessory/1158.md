O Almighty God, who hast made the seas, and all that moveth therein: Bestow thy blessing on the harvest of the waters, that it may be abundant in its season, and on our sailors and fishermen, that they may be safe in every peril of the deep; so that all with thankful heart may acknowledge thee, who art the Lord of the sea and of the dry ground; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
