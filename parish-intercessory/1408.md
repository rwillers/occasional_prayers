Almighty God, to forget whom is to stumble and fall, to remember whom is to rise again: We pray thee to draw the people of this country to thyself; prosper all efforts to make known to them the truth of thy gospel; that many may learn their need of thee and thy love for them, and that so thy Church and kingdom may be established to the glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
