Almighty Father, we pray thee to send thy blessing on those whom thou hast joined together in the fellowship of Guiding in this parish.  Strengthen them to be loyal to their vows and to fulfill faithfully the daily calls of duty; fill them with high ideals; and inspire them with love and goodwill to all mankind, that they may follow in the footsteps of him who died in the service of men, thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
