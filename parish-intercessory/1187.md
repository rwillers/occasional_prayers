O God our Father, as we praise thee for thy goodness and mercy through the past years, so we beseech thee to continue thy work of healing among us.  Grant to physicians, surgeons, nurses, and all who cooperate with them, wisdom and skill, patience, strength and tenderness, that by their endeavour many may be made whole in body and mind; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
