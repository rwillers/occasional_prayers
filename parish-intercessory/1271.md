O God, who knowest the needs of all thy children, look With compassion upon him for whom our prayers are offered; give him courage and confidence; bless those who minister to him of thy healing gifts, and, if it be thy gracious will, restore him to that perfect health which is thine alone to give; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
