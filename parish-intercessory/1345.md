Remember for good, we beseech thee, O Lord, the missionary work of thy Church throughout the world.  Protect and provide for thy servants in the mission fields, and bestow upon them all things needful for their work.  And give to the churches abroad and to us at home such an increased spirit of faith, sacrifice and service, that the work of the gospel may not be hindered, but that in every land thy kingdom may be advanced; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
