Eternal God, who rulest in the kingdoms of men: Grant, we most humbly beseech thee, honour and safety to our sovereign lady the Queen; peace throughout the commonwealth of her peoples; promotion to true religion; encouragement to learning and godly living; a patient service to the concord of the world; and, by all these, glory to thy holy name; for his sake to whom thou hast given all power in heaven and earth, even our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
