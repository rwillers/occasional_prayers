Eternal God, we beseech thee for all who serve in the United Nations Organization.  Grant thy blessing upon their endeavours to heal the wounds of the world and to minister to human need; and so guide their deliberations in council and assembly that all causes of strife may be removed, and peace and concord be secured among the nations of the world; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
