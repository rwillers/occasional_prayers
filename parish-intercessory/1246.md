Heavenly Father, who art the source of all refreshment: Be with us and all who are seeking rest and holiday at this time.  Make us grateful for all thy good gifts which we enjoy; help us to see thee in the beauty and majesty of nature; and grant us on our return renewed strength of mind and body for the duties of our daily life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
