O God, who from time to time dost send a gracious rain upon thine inheritance, refreshing it when it is weary: Hearken now, we beseech thee, to the supplications of thy people, who confess their need of thy quickening grace.  Revive us by the Holy Ghost, the giver of life; that all men may see and know that thou art in the midst of us, who are called by thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
