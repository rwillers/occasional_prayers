O Merciful God, whose blessed Son went about doing good: Uphold with thy strength and grace those who do service to the wounded and the sick.  Grant to the ministers of thy gospel faithfulness and love; to doctors and nurses, skill, sympathy and patience; and to those who labour in the place of danger, thy protection; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
