title: 'The Suffering — Incurables'

O Heavenly Father, we pray thee for those suffering from diseases for which at present there is no cure.  Give them the victory of trust and hope, that they may never lose their faith in thy loving purpose.  Grant thy wisdom to all who are working to discover the causes of disease, and the realization that through thee all things are possible.  We ask this in the name of him who went about doing good and healing all manner of disease, even thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
