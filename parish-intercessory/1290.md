title: 'The Suffering — Alcoholics'
layout: page
tags: ['Intercessory Prayers', 'The Suffering']
source_order: 1290
---

Most gracious God, the strength of all who put their trust in thee: We pray for those who are enslaved by intoxicants or by some other evil habit; give them the desire and the will to be free, and the grace to continue in the right way; and show us how we may serve and help them in their need; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
