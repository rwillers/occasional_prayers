Almighty God our heavenly Father, whose property is always to have mercy: We most humbly beseech thee to enlighten by thy Holy Spirit all who have gone astray from the truth of thy holy Word, and to bring them to a due sense of their error, that they may with hearty faith receive and hold fast thine unchangeable truth; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
