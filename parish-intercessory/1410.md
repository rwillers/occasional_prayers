O God, our heavenly Father, we pray thee to bless the mission to this parish.  Prepare the hearts of the people for thy message, and fill the missioners and those taking part with thy Holy Spirit, that many souls may be won for him who is the Way, the Truth and the Life, thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
