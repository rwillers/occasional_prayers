Have mercy, O Lord our God, on those whom war or oppression or famine has robbed of homes and friends, and prosper all who seek to help them.  We commend also to thy care those whose homes are broken by conflict and lack of love; grant that where the love of man has failed, the divine compassion may heal; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
