Almighty God, who givest wisdom, and rulest the hearts of men: Guide and direct, we beseech thee, the minds of all those who are called to exercise the responsibility of electing fit persons to serve in the parliament of this nation; grant that the issue of their choice may promote thy glory and the welfare of this people; and to those who shall be elected give the spirit of understanding, courage, and true godliness; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
