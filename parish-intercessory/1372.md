O Almighty God, who hast hallowed words to be an instrument of thy Word: We beseech thee to direct and use the labours of all Christian writers and translators, and of those who distribute Christian literature, to the advancement of the knowledge of thy love among the nations of the world, and the building up of thy Church in every land; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
