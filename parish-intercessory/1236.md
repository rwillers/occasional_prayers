---
title: 'Home and Friends — Absent Friends (4)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1236
---

O God, who art present in thy power in every place, mercifully hear our prayers for those we love who are now parted from us.  Watch over them, we beseech thee, and protect them in all anxiety, danger and temptation; and teach us and them to feel and know that thou art always near, and that we are one in thee for ever; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
