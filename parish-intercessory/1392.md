O God, who hast called us to be members together of thy Church, which is the body of thy Son: Pour out, we beseech thee, thy Spirit on this diocese.  Endue with strength and love and wisdom all who bear authority therein.  Accept our gifts, our labours, and our love, and use them for the advancement of thy kingdom and the glory of thy name.  Lead us in all our work for thee, and unite us in the joyful service of thy Son, our Saviour Jesus Christ.
