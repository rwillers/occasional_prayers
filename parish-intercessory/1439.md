Heavenly Father, who hast called thy Church to worship thee and to bear witness for thee in the world: Make us faithful to our calling; that offering to thee the spiritual sacrifice of our praise and thanksgiving, we may preach the faith of Christ crucified, in the power of the Holy Spirit; through the same Jesus Christ thy Son, our Saviour.
