O Holy Father, from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named: We commend to thy gracious keeping the homes and children of our people; beseeching thee so to dwell in our hearts that we may know in daily life, and show forth to all men, the power and depth of thy grace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
