O most Holy Trinity of perfect love, come in thy power upon thy whole Church, and especially upon those who serve thee in this parish; that in all their life and labour for thy kingdom they may always be guided, strengthened and upheld by thy peace and thy presence; who livest and reignest one God, world without end.
