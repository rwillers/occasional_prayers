Make thyself known, O Christ, throughout the world which thou didst die to save.  Reveal thyself among all nations, amid all people, and across all barriers.  Renew thy Church with love and power, that the kingdoms of this world may become thine own; who with the Father and the Holy Spirit livest and reignest one God, for ever and ever.
