---
title: 'The Nations of the World — The Peace of the World (8)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers, The Nation]
source_order: 1054
---

O Thou Prince of Peace, shed abroad thy peace upon the world.  By the might of thy Holy Spirit quench the pride and anger and greediness which cause man to strive against man, and people against people.  Lead all nations in the ways of mutual help and goodwill, and hasten the time when the earth shall confess thee for its Saviour and King; for thy holy name’s sake.
