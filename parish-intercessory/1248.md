title: 'Travel — Valedictory Prayer'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1248
---

O God, who art the refuge and strength of those who trust in thee: We commend to thy merciful care thy servants now departing from us.  Prosper them, we pray thee, in all their undertakings; guide and protect them in the way that they go; and of thy great goodness bring them again to their homes and friends in peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
