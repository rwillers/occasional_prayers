title: 'The Parish — Our Christian Witness (5)'
layout: page
tags: ['Intercessory Prayers', 'The Parish']
source_order: 1427
---

Almighty Father, who in thy redeeming love hast drawn us together as members of thy Church in this parish: Deepen our love for thee, we pray thee, and strengthen our fellowship one with another; and grant that by the witness of our corporate life others within this place may be brought to the knowledge of thee, and thy blessed kingdom may be enlarged; for the glory of Jesus Christ our Lord.
