Bless, O Lord, we beseech thee, our Queen and parliament, our judges and magistrates, and all who bear any office or rule in the state.  Save us from the sins that most easily beset us; bless every effort for the well-being of our people; and make us a nation fearing thee and doing righteousness, to the glory of thy holy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
