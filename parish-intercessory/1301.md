Most merciful and faithful High Priest, who didst deign for us to be tempted of the devil: Make speed to aid thy servants who are assaulted by manifold temptations; and as thou knowest their several infirmities, let each one find thee mighty to save; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Ghost, one God, world without end.
