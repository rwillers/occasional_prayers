Almighty God, our heavenly Father, bless our country and commonwealth, that we may be made a blessing to the world.  Grant us sound government and just laws, good education and a clean press, simplicity and justice in our relations with one another, and, above all, a spirit of service which will abolish pride of place and inequality of opportunity; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
