O Lord Jesus Christ, who by thy cross and resurrection hast overcome the powers of darkness: Guide all men who in seeking knowledge acquire power which can be used for good or evil purposes.  Help them to recognize their stewardship and to fulfill their responsibility as in thy sight; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
