Lord, who hast given us this land for our abiding place: Help us to love it with a passion so strong and true that we may be jealous for its honour and instant in its service; and we pray thee so to work through us that it may become a land where men walk in the freedom of the truth and in the light of the knowledge of thy love, which thou hast manifested in thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
