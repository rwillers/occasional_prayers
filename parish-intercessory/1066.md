Grant, O Lord God, that laying aside the sins that hinder the coming of thy kingdom, the nations of the world may be assured of the right which thou hast given them to live in freedom; freedom from want and fear, freedom of thought and speech, and freedom to worship thee according to thy will; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
