We commend to thee, Almighty God, the whole Christian Church throughout the world.  Bless all in every place who call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.  May the grace and power of the Holy Spirit fill every member, so that all the company of thy faithful people may bear witness for thee on the earth.  If it be good in thy sight, heal the outward divisions of thy people, disposing the wills of all to a true union of order in the truth, for the work of the one Lord.  And above all we pray for the unity of the Spirit, through whom alone we are guided into all truth; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
