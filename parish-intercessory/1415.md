God the Father, God the Son, God the Holy Ghost, bless, preserve and keep you.  The love of God the Father encompass you.  The presence of God the Son uphold you.  The power of God the Spirit rest upon you.  Go forth in the name of God, as ambassadors of Christ and servants of his holy Church.  And may the Lord bless your labours abundantly, for the glory of the same our Saviour Jesus Christ.
