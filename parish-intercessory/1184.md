title: 'Health Services and Hospitals — Doctors and Nurses (2)'

source_order: 1184

O Heavenly Father, who by thy Son Jesus Christ hast taught us the glory of self-sacrifice and service: Bless all those who are called to care for the sick in mind and body; may they always remember that they are fellow-workers with thee; and give them such patience, skill and love that they may bring healing and comfort to those whom they serve; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
