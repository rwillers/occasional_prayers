O God, our heavenly Father, guide with thy Holy Spirit those who direct the commercial life of the nation.  Grant them to look on their life’s work as an opportunity of service rather than of gain; fill them with jealous care of their employees; and so bless them that the whole nation may be better for their labours; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
