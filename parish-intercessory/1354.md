Lord of all power and might, who hast commanded thy Church to preach the Word and make disciples of all nations: Inflame us with the fire of thy love, that we may give ourselves, our time and our money, as faithful stewards of the gospel, to the honour and glory of thy holy name; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
