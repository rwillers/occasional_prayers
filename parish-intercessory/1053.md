Inspire, we beseech thee, O Lord, the hearts of all men everywhere with the spirit of love and justice; and enlighten the leaders of the nations with the wisdom which is from above, that they may seek first the coming of thy kingdom.  Deliver us thy people from faithlessness and fear, save us from the sins and cruelties of war, and guide our feet into thy way of peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
