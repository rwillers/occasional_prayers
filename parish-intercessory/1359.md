Almighty God, who by thy Son Jesus Christ didst give commandment to the apostles that they should go into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature: Raise up, we beseech thee, true and faithful men to continue that work in this our day, that they may seek and find thy sheep dispersed and lost, and bring them home to thy flock; and grant us grace to labour with them by our prayers and offerings for the furtherance of thy kingdom among men; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
