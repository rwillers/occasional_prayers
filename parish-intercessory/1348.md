O God, our heavenly Father, as we thank thee for thy love to us and above all for the gift of thy dear Son to die for us, we pray thee for all who do not know thy love and have never heard of our Saviour Jesus Christ.  Bless all who work in other lands as messengers of thy truth; and help us here at home by our prayers and offerings to hasten the coming of the kingdom of thy love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
