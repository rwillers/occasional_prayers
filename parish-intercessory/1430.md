Bless, we beseech thee, O Lord, the clergy of this parish, and strengthen them with thy heavenly grace.  May they be diligent in prayer and in the study of thy holy Word, that by wise counsel and good example they may guide thy people committed to their charge, and labour with unwearied zeal for the extension of thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
