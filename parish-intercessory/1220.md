Sanctify, O Lord, the homes of our people, that they may be places of prayer and purity and true religion.  Knit together in constant love those whom thou hast made one in holy wedlock.  Bestow upon children the dew of thy blessing, that they may grow up in the faith of thy name and in the spirit of love and service; for the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
