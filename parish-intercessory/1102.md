Graciously hear us, O heavenly Father, for those who guard the health of the nation, that they may preserve us from sickness and disease; for those who guide the thought of the nation, that they may inspire us with noble and worthy ideals; for those who mould the character of the nation, that they may fashion us into a people strong in faith and righteousness; to the glory of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
