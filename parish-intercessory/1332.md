---
title: 'The Church of God — The Unity of the Church (6)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers, The Church]
source_order: 1332
---

O God, who art the Father of all, and who alone makest men to be of one mind in a house; We beseech thee to grant to us, by the inspiration of thy Holy Spirit, a fuller realization of our brotherhood in thee.  Enlarge our sympathies; remove all prejudice and bitterness; and deepen our understanding one of another; for the sake of thy Son our Lord Jesus Christ.
