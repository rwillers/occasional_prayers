---
title: 'The Suffering — The Sick in Body (5)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1268
---

O Almighty God, who art the giver of life and health and the aid of all who seek thy succour: Mercifully grant that thy help and goodness may be shown to the suffering and the sick; that being healed of their infirmities they may give thanks to thee in thy holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
