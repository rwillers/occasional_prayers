Almighty God our heavenly Father, we pray thee to multiply thy heavenly gifts upon thy servant Elizabeth our Queen.  Establish her throne in righteousness, uphold her continually with thy strength and guidance, and make her a blessing to the peoples over whom thou hast called her to rule; for the honour of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
