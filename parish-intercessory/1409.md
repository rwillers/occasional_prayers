title: 'Evangelism — A Parish Mission (1)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1409
---

O God, send thy blessing upon the mission to be held in this place; and grant that Jesus Christ may be so presented in the power of the Holy Spirit that many may come to put their trust in thee through him, to accept him as their Saviour and serve him as their King in the fellowship of his Church; for the honour of thy holy name.
