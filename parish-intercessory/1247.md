Almighty God, from whom all good things do come, protect and bless, we beseech thee, all who, released for a season from their daily tasks, are enjoying the rest and refreshment of their annual holidays; and grant that, renewed in body and spirit, they may return home better able to serve thee and their fellow men; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
