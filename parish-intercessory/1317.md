Remember in thy mercy, O Lord our God, thy holy Church throughout the world.  Heal its divisions; restore its unity; quicken its life; empower its witness; that so it may become the instrument of thy purpose for the reconciliation of mankind and the healing of the nations; through our Saviour Jesus Christ.
