O God our Father, who hast led our nation wondrously in ages past: We thank thee for the guidance of thy overruling providence; and we pray thee to give us now and in the days to come sure confidence in thee, that in all perplexities we may see thy light, and in hours of weakness may know thy strength; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
