O Lord and Master Jesus Christ, Word of the everlasting Father, who hast borne our griefs and carried the burden of our infirmities: Renew by thy Holy Spirit in thy Church, we beseech thee, thy gifts of healing, and send forth thy disciples again to preach the gospel of thy kingdom, and to cure the sick and relieve thy suffering children, to the praise and glory of thy holy name.
