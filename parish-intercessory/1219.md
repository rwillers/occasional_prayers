---
title: 'Home and Friends — Family Life (6)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1219
---

Almighty God, our heavenly Father, whose blessed Son did share at Nazareth the life of an earthly home: We beseech thee to bless our homes, and our families, and to keep us in our going out and in our coming in.  Grant us day by day thy strength and protection; watch over us in times of danger and necessity; and unite us with each other in thy steadfast fear and love; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
