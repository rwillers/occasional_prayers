O God, who hast made of one blood all the nations of the earth, and hast set the bounds of their habitation that they might seek after thee and find thee: Mercifully hear our supplications, and remove from us the menace of war.  Guide the rulers with thy counsel and restrain the passions of the people, so that bloodshed may be averted and peace be preserved; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
