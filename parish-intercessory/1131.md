Almighty God, Father of all mankind, who hast given to our nation a place of responsibility in the earth: Help us ever to remember that we are stewards of thy gifts.  Give to us the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and of the fear of the Lord; make us just and fair in our dealings with other peoples; and keep the nations of our commonwealth united in one bond of faith and fellowship; to the honour of thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
