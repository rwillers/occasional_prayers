---
title: 'The Church of God — The Unity of the Church (1)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers, The Church]
source_order: 1327
---

Almighty God, who art one Lord, and hast called us to glorify thee as one body in Christ: We earnestly pray for the restoration of the visible unity of the catholic Church; that all who confess thy holy name may be reunited, as at the beginning, in the apostles’ teaching and fellowship, in the breaking of bread and in prayers.  Remove from us and from others all suspicions and prejudices and whatever may hinder thy purpose; and endue us with such love towards thee and towards one another that we may be one in thee; to whom be all praise and glory, now and for evermore.
