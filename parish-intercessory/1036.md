O God, to whom we pray for the coming of thy kingdom: Let this be no vain repetition on our part.  Give us the zeal that will not rest till it sees thy kingdom on earth.  Make us to hate all evil things that are contrary to thy will and to fight against them in thy name.  May we ever be found working for thee, hastening the time when the earth shall be filled with the knowledge of thy love; for the glory of Jesus Christ our Lord.
