Lord Jesus Christ, who didst behold with love the young ruler who came to thee and didst call him to follow thee: We pray that young people everywhere may hear and obey thy call; and grant them such a vision of thy loving purpose for the world that they may surrender themselves gladly and unreservedly to thyself for the service of thy kingdom.  We ask this for the honour of thy holy name.
