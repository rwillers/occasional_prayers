Almighty God, our heavenly Father, bless, we pray thee, our work which we now present and dedicate to thee for the extension of thy kingdom in the world; and make us so thankful for the precious gift to us of thy beloved Son, that we may pray fervently, labour diligently, and give liberally to make him known to all nations as their Saviour and King; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
