Eternal God, wonderful in counsel, excellent in wisdom, we pray for the representatives of the nations who have been called to the difficult task of laying firm foundations for peace.  Inspire their minds, enlarge their vision, direct their councils; that wisely and fearlessly they may stand for righteousness and truth, and set forward thy purposes for the welfare of mankind; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
