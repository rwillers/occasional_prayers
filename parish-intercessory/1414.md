O Heavenly Father, prosper with thy blessing, we pray thee, the mission to be held in this parish, and use it for the winning of many souls to Christ and his Church.  Prepare the hearts of all to hear thy Word; give thy grace to the missioner and to those who work with him; and show us each one what thou wouldest have us to do; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Saviour.
