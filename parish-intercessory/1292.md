---
title: 'The Suffering — The Anxious and Bereaved'
layout: page
tags: ['Intercessory Prayers', 'The Suffering']
source_order: 1292
---

Almighty God, who art afflicted in the afflictions of thy people: Regard with thy tender compassion the anxious and bereaved.  Bear their sorrows and cares; give them comfort and peace in thee; and grant them evermore sure trust and confidence in thy fatherly care: through Jesus Christ our Lord.
