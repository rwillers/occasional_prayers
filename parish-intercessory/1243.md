---
title: 'Travel — Travellers on the Roads (2)'
layout: page
tags: ['Intercessory Prayers', 'Travel']
source_order: 1243
---

Almighty God, who dost sanctify with thy Spirit the common ways of life: Give to us and to all who use the roads the spirit of courtesy and goodwill, of carefulness and self-control; that by our thought for others we may all be preserved from needless danger and sudden death, and may live to glorify thee in our going out and our coming in; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
