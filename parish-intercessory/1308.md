title: 'The Church of God (4)'
attribution: Gelasian Sacramentary

source_order: 1308

O God, Redeemer and Governor of mankind: We beseech thee to grant that thy Church may continually be enlarged by the gathering in of new children to thee, and perfected by the increasing devotion of those who have been regenerated; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
