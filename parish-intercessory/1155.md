O God, whose Son was content to share the life of his village at Nazareth: Bless, we beseech thee, the life of this village with thy continual presence.  Grant that in every family thy name may be hallowed, and thy will be done; that our children may learn to love the faith of Jesus Christ, and that men and women may be good neighbours and live godly lives; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
