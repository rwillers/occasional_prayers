Almighty and everlasting God, who art King of kings and Lord of lords: Guide, we pray thee, the destinies of our commonwealth of nations.  Direct the counsels of our Queen and all in authority under her.  Strengthen the resolve of our people; deepen the sense of unity among the members of this great family; and grant us grace that we may strive manfully under thy banner for the establishment of righteousness and peace among the nations of the world; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
