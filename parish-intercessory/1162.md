O God, by whose providence the duties of man are variously ordered: Grant to us all in this school such a spirit that we may labour heartily to do our work in our several stations.  Teach us to put to good account whatever talents thou hast lent us, that as good and faithful servants we may enter into the joy of our Lord, even thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
