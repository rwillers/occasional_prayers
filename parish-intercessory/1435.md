O Lord, without whom our labour is but lost, and with whom thy little ones go forth as the mighty: Be present to all works in thy Church which are undertaken according to thy will; and grant to thy labourers a pure intention, patient faith, sufficient success upon earth, and the bliss of serving thee in heaven; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
