---
title: 'In Time of War — On Active Service (3)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1206
---

O Lord God, our Father, our Saviour, our might: We commend to thy keeping all those who are venturing their lives on our behalf; that, whether by life or by death, they may win for the whole world the fruits of their sacrifice, and a righteous and abiding peace; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
