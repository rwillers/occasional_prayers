---
title: 'Home and Friends — Children (3)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1225
---

O God our Father, we pray thee to bless all children, and to give to those who have the care of them wisdom, patience, and love; so that the home in which they grow up may be to them an image of thy kingdom, and the care of their parents a likeness of thy love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
