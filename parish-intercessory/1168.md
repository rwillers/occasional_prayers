Let thy Spirit, O God, rest upon all who teach in our schools.  Enable them both by word and by example to lead their scholars to reverence truth, to desire goodness, and to delight in beauty; that, following after these things, both those who learn and those who teach may come to know and worship thee, the giver of all that is good; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
