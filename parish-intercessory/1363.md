We commend to thy mercy, O Lord our God, thy servants who are about to return to the work to which thou hast called them.  As they go forth again in obedience to thy call, so may they go in thy strength and under thy care.  Guard and guide them in all their work for thee; keep them in health of body and peace of mind; and use them abundantly for the furtherance of the gospel and the glory of thy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
