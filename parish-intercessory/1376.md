Almighty God, who dost reveal thyself to us through the written Word: We thank thee for all who have worked so that we may know that Word in our own tongue.  We pray thee to inspire with thy Holy Spirit all who are now translating the Scriptures into other languages, that by thy blessing upon their work the Word of the Lord may have free course and be glorified; through Jesus Christ our Saviour.
