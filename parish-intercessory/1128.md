O Lord our God, who art the refuge and strength of all who put their trust in thee: We give thee our humble and hearty thanks for the deliverance granted to our nation in the Battle of Britain, and for those who by their sacrifice won that deliverance for us.  Teach us, we pray thee, as we honour their memory and call to mind thy great mercy, to renew the dedication of our lives to thy service, and always to use our freedom for thy glory; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
