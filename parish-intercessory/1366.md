---
title: 'The Church Overseas — Those Young in the Faith'
layout: page
tags: ['Intercessory Prayers', 'The Church Overseas']
source_order: 1366
---

O Lord Jesus Christ, who art the Way, the Truth and the Life: Bless and build up in the fellowship of thy Church all who are young in the faith.  Deliver them from idolatry, superstition and fear; instruct them in the truth of thy holy gospel; endue them with constancy, and establish them in holiness and righteousness; for the honour and glory of thy name.
