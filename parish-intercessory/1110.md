O Lord most high, who alone rulest in the kingdoms of men: Guide and direct, we beseech thee, the minds of those who are about to exercise their responsibility in the election of fit persons to serve in the ——; and we pray thee in thy good providence to overrule all things for the promotion of thy glory and the welfare of this nation \[or community\]; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
