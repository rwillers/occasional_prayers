O God, who by thy Son Jesus Christ didst command thine apostles to preach the gospel to every creature: Prosper, we pray thee all missions of thy Church.  Send forth labourers into thy harvest, and grant such success to their labours that thy kingdom may be extended among the nations, till the whole earth be filled with the knowledge of thy love; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
