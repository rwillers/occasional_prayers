title: 'The Church of God (11)'
layout: page

O Lord God, we pray for thy whole Church throughout the world.  May her witness be faithful and her worship sincere; may her divisions be healed and her life renewed; and may all her members be bound together in loving service to one another and in true devotion to thee; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
