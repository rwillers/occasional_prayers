O Lord Jesus Christ, physician of men and nations, who didst show thy love by healing all manner of sickness and disease: Bless, we beseech thee, all doctors and nurses who are serving in missionary hospitals and dispensaries, and all who are preparing for this service.  Give to them the joy of dedicated lives which reveal thy power and thy love; for thy name’s sake.
