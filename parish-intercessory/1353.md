O Most merciful Father, we confess that we have done little to forward thy kingdom in the world and to advance thy glory.  Pardon, we pray thee, our shortcomings; give us greater zeal for thy glory; and make us more ready and diligent by our prayers, our gifts and our example to spread abroad the knowledge of thy truth and to enlarge the boundaries of thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
