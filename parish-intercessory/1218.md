Almighty Father, who art ever present with us in all the duties of our daily life: Be thyself, we beseech thee, the defender of our households and our constant guide; and as thy Son, Jesus Christ, abode on earth in his home at Nazareth, so let him now abide with us and we with him; that continuing ever in his love we may love one another in him; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
