Lord of the nations, who hast broken down in Christ the wall of partition between Jew and Gentile, bond and free: Unite the Christian people of different races in the bonds of true brotherhood; so that East and West, made one in Christ, may together labour for the bringing in of thy kingdom; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
