---
title: 'In Time of War — War Victims (1)'
attribution: Frederick B. Macnutt
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1209
---

O God, who hast promised that they that wait upon thee shall renew their strength: We commend to thee all who suffer in this time of war; the wounded, the sick, and the prisoners; the homeless, the hungry, and the oppressed; the anxious, the frightened, and the bereaved.  Strengthen them, O Lord, with thy Holy Spirit, and give them friends to help them; we ask it in his name, who bore for us the agony of the cross, thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.
