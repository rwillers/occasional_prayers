O God, who art the Father of all, and who alone makest men to be of one mind in a house: We beseech thee, at this time of strife and unrest, to grant to us by the inspiration of thy Holy Spirit a fuller realization of our brotherhood, man with man.  Allay all anger and bitterness, and deepen in us a sense of truth and equity in our dealings one with another; for the sake of thy Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.
