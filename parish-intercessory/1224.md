O Lord Jesus Christ, who didst take little children in thine arms and bless them: Bless, we beseech thee, all children dear to us.  Take them into the arms of thy everlasting mercy, keep them from all evil, and bring them into the company of those who ever behold the face of thy Father in heaven; to the glory of thy holy name.
