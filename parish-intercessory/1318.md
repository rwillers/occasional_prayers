O Most Holy Spirit of God, from whom alone floweth the fullness of wisdom and life: Come in thine everlasting power and glory, we beseech thee, upon thy Church, and into the hearts of all men, to bring to the world a new birth of righteousness, new understanding of truth, and new unity in love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
