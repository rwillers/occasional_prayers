---
title: 'The Suffering — Those in Need (8)'
layout: page
tags: [Intercessory Prayers]
source_order: 1257
---

O God, remember in thy mercy the poor and needy, the widow and the fatherless, the stranger and the friendless, the sick and the dying.  Relieve their needs, sanctify their sufferings, uphold their faith, and in due time bring them out of all their trouble; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
