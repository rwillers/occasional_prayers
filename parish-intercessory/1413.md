Almighty Father, whose blessed Son came to seek and to save that which was lost: We pray thee to use for thine own glory the mission to be held in this parish.  Prepare and empower thy servant the missioner; revive and sanctify thy people; and bring many souls to a knowledge of thyself; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
