Almighty God, who hast created man in thine own image: Grant us grace fearlessly to contend against evil, and to make no peace with oppression; and, that we may reverently use our freedom, help us to employ it in the maintaining of justice among men and nations, to the glory of thy holy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
