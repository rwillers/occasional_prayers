O God, who hast graciously preserved our nation through the years and hast led us in wondrous ways: Grant that we may be worthy of our high calling.  Purge out from among us the sins that dishonour thee.  Give us true religion; crown our faith with righteousness; and lift us up, a holy people, to thy praise and honour; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
