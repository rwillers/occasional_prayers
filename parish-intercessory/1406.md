Merciful and gracious Father, who wouldest have all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth: Stir in the hearts of the clergy a desire to do the work of an evangelist and to make full proof of their ministry; that so those who are as yet unconverted may hear thy summons to choose whom they will serve and may own thee Lord of all; for Jesus Christ’s sake.
