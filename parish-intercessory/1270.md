Grant, O Lord, to all those who are bearing pain, thy healing grace and power; thy spirit of peace and hope, of courage and endurance.  Cast out from them the spirit of anxiety and fear; and grant them perfect confidence and trust in thee; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
