Almighty God, at whose call thy servant Andrew left all to follow Christ, and by whose grace he was made strong for service: Help us, whom also thou hast called to be thy servants, so to persevere in the way of faith and duty, that finally we may finish our course with joy; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
