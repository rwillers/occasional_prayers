O Almighty God our heavenly Father, whose blessed Son was circumcised and kept the law for us, that we might enter into the glorious liberty of the sons of God: Grant us grace to bring forth the fruits of his righteousness, and ever more to serve thee with pure hearts and holy lives; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
