O God, who through the example and teaching of thy Son hast shown to us the royal path of service: Help us to serve others in all humility and patient love; that following in the steps of thy holy apostles we may be made worthy to inherit thy everlasting kingdom; through our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
