O Son of God, who by the travail of thy passion hast brought to birth the new family of the children of God: Grant us grace, as members of that redeemed family, to realize our kinship one with another in thee, and to love all men for thy sake, who hast loved us and given thyself for us, our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
