Lord Jesus, who in Capernaum didst heal the sufferer borne of four: Behold in thy mercy those whom no man can cure, and whom in prayer we lay at thy feet.  O thou with whom all things are possible, grant them thy healing and help, and answer our prayer according to thy holy will; for the glory of thy great name.
