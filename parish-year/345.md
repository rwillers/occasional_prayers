Almighty God, Lord of Heaven and earth: We beseech thee to pour forth thy blessing upon this land, and to give us fruitful seasons; that we, constantly receiving thy bounty, may evermore give thanks unto thee in thy holy Church; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
