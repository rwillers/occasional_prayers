Glory to our ascended Lord, that he is with us always.<br>Glory to the Word of God, going forth with his armies, conquering and to conquer.<br>Glory to him who has led captivity captive, and given gifts for the perfecting of his saints.<br>Glory to him who has gone before to prepare a place in his Father’s home for us.<br>Glory to the author and finisher of our faith; that God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory and dominion now and for evermore.
