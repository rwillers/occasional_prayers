O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the most high God, who didst empty thyself and give thy whole life to us, even unto death, the death of the cross: Grant us to receive so immeasurable a gift penitently, gladly and thankfully; and to hold back nothing of ourselves from others and from thee; who livest and reignest in the glory of the eternal Trinity, God for ever and ever.
