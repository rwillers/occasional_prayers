Grant, O Lord, we beseech thee, that as thy Son Jesus Christ, after he had suffered in the flesh for our sins, did preach to the spirits in prison, so also thy Church may ever be ready in body and spirit to do thy will; that by the preaching of thy gospel all who shall be obedient to the faith may be gathered into thy glorious kingdom; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
