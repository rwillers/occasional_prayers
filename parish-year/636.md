We commend to thee, O God, all those whom thou hast called to the sacred ministry of thy Church.  Give them grace to be faithful ambassadors of Christ to the world, to feed and govern thy flock as true shepherds, and to promote love and unity among all thy people; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
