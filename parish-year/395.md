Almighty God, who according to promise didst pour out thy Holy Spirit on the day of Pentecost, and hast promised that he shall abide with thy Church for ever: Let that same Spirit, we pray thee, guide us into all truth, defend us from all evil, and enrich us with all grace; that henceforth we may serve thee with pure hearts and lives, to the honour of thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
