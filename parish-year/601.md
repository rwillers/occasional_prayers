O Lord, who by thy mercy hast brought us to the beginning of harvest and to the remembrance of Saint Peter’s deliverance: Let his counsel prevail among us, we beseech thee; that girding up the loins of our minds as of our bodies, we may set our hope perfectly on the grace that is to be brought to us by the revelation of thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
