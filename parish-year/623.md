Grant thy grace, O God our Father, to those who are training for the ministry of thy holy Church.  Prepare them for all that thou hast prepared for them; make them what thou wouldest have them to be, that they may accomplish what thou wouldest have them to do; and so bless them that they may be made a blessing; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
