O God, who by the preaching of thy blessed servant David didst cause the light of the gospel to shine in an age of darkness: Grant, we beseech thee, that having his life and his labours in remembrance, we may show forth our thankfulness by following the example of his zeal and patience; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
