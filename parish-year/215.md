Glory be to thee, O Lord, who, having become obedient unto death, even the death of the cross, art highly exalted, and hast opened unto us the gate of everlasting life.  Therefore unto thee every knee shall bow, and every tongue shall confess thee Lord, to the glory of God the Father.
