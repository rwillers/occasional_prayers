O Lord Jesus Christ, who from the cross didst commend thy mother into the care of the beloved disciple: Keep, we beseech thee, under thy perpetual providence those to whom we are bound by ties of kindred and affection; and grant that the love which we know on earth may lead us onward to the love of thee; who with the Father and the eternal Spirit art one holy Trinity of love, world without end.
