O God, who madest Saint David to be a victorious champion of the faith, and didst reward his rich sowing with an abundant harvest; Continue thy favour, we beseech thee, towards the people of Wales and the Church in their midst; and so bless them with spiritual gifts that the land of their fathers may be a praise in all the earth; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
