We pray, O Lord, for thy blessing upon all who are being trained for the ministry of thy Church in every land.  Take from them all pride and self-conceit, all thought of worldly advancement.  May their wills be wholly surrendered unto thee; fill them with thy Spirit, that they may go forth inspired with zeal for thy glory, in the power of our Lord Jesus Christ.
