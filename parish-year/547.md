O Lord, on this day when we remember how innocent children suffered death for the sake of thy holy Son: We beseech thee to slay in us everything that displeases thee and contradicts thy perfect will; that we who have been called to be thy children may glorify thee in our lives; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
