O Lord Jesus Christ, who after thy resurrection from the dead didst gloriously ascend into heaven: Grant us the aid of thy loving-kindness, that according to thy promise thou mayest ever dwell with us on earth, and we with thee in heaven, where with the Father and the Holy Spirit thou livest and reignest one God for ever and ever.
