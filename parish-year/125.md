O God most High, who alone rulest in the kingdoms of men: Grant, we beseech thee, to all members of Parliament the light and guidance of thy Holy Spirit; that they may wisely take counsel together, and come to such decisions as shall promote thy glory, the well-being of this nation, and the peace of the world; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
