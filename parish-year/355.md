O God, Father of all men, we pray for all who toil in mill or mine; for all by whose labour we are clothed and fed; for all who work in the darkness of the earth; for those who trade in shop or market; and for those who go down to the sea in ships and do business in great waters.  May our service and our merchandise be holy unto the Lord; and may we do justly, love mercy, and walk humbly with thee; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
