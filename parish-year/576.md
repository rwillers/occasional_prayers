We give thee thanks, Almighty Father, for the angelic hosts around thy throne who offer thee their tireless service and unending praise; and we pray that we on earth, like them, may readily fulfill thy commandments and render thee the glory that is thy due; for the worthiness of thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
