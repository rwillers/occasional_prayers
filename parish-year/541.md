Almighty God, who to thy holy Apostle Thomas didst reveal thine incarnate Son in his risen glory: Draw, we beseech thee, the people of our land to know and confess him as their Lord and God, that coming to thee by him they may believe and have life in thy name: through the same Jesus Christ our Lord and Saviour.
