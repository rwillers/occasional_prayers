Pour out, O Lord, we beseech thee, the Spirit of power upon all those whom thou hast called to be preachers of the Word.  Inflame them with the fire of thy love; grant them new tongues with which to move the hearts of men; give them utterance to meet the needs of the day and the hour; that so believers may be added to thy Church, multitudes both of men and women; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
