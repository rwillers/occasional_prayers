O Lord Jesus, who dost set over thy people the pastors and teachers of thy choice: Give us, we beseech thee, childlike hearts; that in humility we may receive thy Word, and in simplicity obey it; who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Ghost, one God, world without end.
