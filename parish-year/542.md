O Lord Jesus Christ, who didst say to thy servant Thomas the Apostle, I am the Way, and the Truth, and the Life: Mercifully grant that we, looking to thee by faith, may find in thee the Way that leads to the Father, the Truth that makes us free, and the Life that is life indeed, now and always.
