O Lord and Master, who didst say to thine apostle Philip, He who has seen me has seen the Father: Reveal thyself to our hearts, we humbly beseech thee, and so open the eyes of our understanding that in thy face we may behold the light of the knowledge of the glory of God; for thy holy name’s sake.
