We beseech thee, O Lord our God, to enlighten the understanding and to strengthen the wills of all who are preparing for the ministry of thy holy Church; that they may readily acknowledge thy claims, wholeheartedly believe thy truth, and cheerfully obey thy calling, for the glory of thy great name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
