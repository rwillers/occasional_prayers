O Lord Jesus Christ, who by thy holy incarnation didst enter our human family, and on the cross didst commend thy mother to the care of a beloved disciple: Purify our homes by thy abiding presence; sanctify all our human relationships; and grant that united one with another in thee, we may together attain thy heavenly kingdom; for the honour of thy great name.
