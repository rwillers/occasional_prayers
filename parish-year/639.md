Almighty God, who hast committed to men and women the ministry of the gospel: Pour thy grace upon thy servants called to the order of deaconesses; that in all that they do and teach they may love and serve thee, to the glory of thy name and the benefit of thy Church; through thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
