O Lord Jesus Christ, who art the Way, the Truth and the Life; we pray thee suffer us not to stray from thee who art the Way, nor to distrust thee who art the Truth, nor to rest in any other thing than thee, who art the Life.  Teach us by thy Holy Spirit what to believe, what to do, and wherein to take our rest.  For thine own name’s sake we ask it.
