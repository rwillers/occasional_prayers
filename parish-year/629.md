Almighty God, grant, we beseech thee, to all whom thou hast called to the sacred ministry of thy Church such a sense of their high calling that they may count no sacrifice too great to make in thy service; that so, bringing a blessing to their people, they may themselves be blessed of thee; for Jesus Christ’s sake.
