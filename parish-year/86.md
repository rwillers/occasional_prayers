O Heavenly Father, whose blessed Son, that he might keep the law which he came to fulfill, received as on this day the outward circumcision: Cleanse our minds by the inward circumcision from all incentives to sin, that we may worship thee in spirit and glory in the same Christ Jesus, now and for evermore.
