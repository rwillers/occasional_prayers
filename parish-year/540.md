O Almighty God, who hast called thy Church to bear witness for thee before all nations: Grant that as Saint Andrew brought his brother to Jesus, so we, like him, may be wise to win souls, and may labour diligently for the enlargement of thy kingdom; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
