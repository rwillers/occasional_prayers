Almighty God, who in every age hast drawn men to be ministers in the apostolic Church: Make known thy will to those whom thou wouldest use today.  Grant them grace to discern, courage to respond, and firm resolve to obey, that the promise of the gospel may be fulfilled in the lives of all people; through the power of Jesus Christ our Lord.
