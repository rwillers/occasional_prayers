Thanks be unto thee, O Christ, because thou hast broken for us the bonds of sin and brought us into fellowship with the Father.<br>Thanks be unto thee, O Christ, because thou hast overcome death and opened to us the gates of eternal life.<br>Thanks be unto thee, O Christ, because where two or three are gathered together in thy Name there art thou in the midst of them.<br>Thanks be unto thee, O Christ, because thou ever livest to make intercession for us.<br>For these and all other benefits of thy mighty resurrection, thanks be unto thee O Christ.
