O Lord Jesus Christ, Son of the living God, who at the ninth hour of the day, with outstretched hands and bowed head, didst commend thy spirit to God the Father, and by thy death unlock the gates of paradise: Mercifully grant that in the hour of our death our souls may come to the true paradise, which is thyself; who livest and reignest God, world without end.
