O God of Abraham, of Isaac and of Jacob, have mercy, we beseech thee, upon thine ancient people the Jews. Deliver them from their hardness of heart and unbelief of thy gospel; that being turned to thee in living faith, they may look upon him whom they pierced, and be brought into thy holy Church; through the same Jesus Christ our Saviour.
