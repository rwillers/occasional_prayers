Almighty God, whose Son our Saviour Jesus Christ took upon him our flesh that he might bear our sins in his own body on the tree: Mercifully grant that we may be counted worthy to have part both in the fellowship of his sufferings and in the glory of his resurrection; to whom with thee and the Holy Ghost be honour and glory, world without end.
