Heavenly Father, by whose grace thy servant John Mark was enabled to rise above failure and to prove himself useful in thy service: Grant us that same grace, we humbly beseech thee; that abiding in Christ we may be steadfast in our faith and fruitful in every good work; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
