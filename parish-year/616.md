O God, you have told us to pray for more labourers in your harvest-field.  We ask you then to call many of your sons to that work and make them ready to hear and act upon your call.  Make them at all times sensitive to your guidance, willing to learn and eager to obey.  We ask this in the name of him who came not to be ministered to but to minister, Jesus Christ your Son our Lord.
