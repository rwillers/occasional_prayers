O Almighty God, who through the holy Apostle Saint John hast revealed to us the mystery of the Word made flesh: Keep us steadfast in the faith that the Son of God has come; that believing in him we may have life through his name; to whom with thee and the Holy Spirit be glory for ever and ever.
