Almighty Father, whose blessed Son by his one perfect and sufficient sacrifice has opened for us a new and living way to thy presence: Grant that, drawing near to thee with a true heart in full assurance of faith, we may find cleansing and a quiet conscience; and give us grace, we pray thee, evermore to hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering, and to serve one another in love and good works; through the merits of the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
