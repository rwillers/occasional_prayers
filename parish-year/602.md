O God, who hast made heaven and earth and all that is in them: We humbly beseech thee to bless and hallow these first-fruits of corn, and to multiply them abundantly for us who offer them; and grant such seasonable weather that this year’s harvest may be plentiful, and we, rejoicing in thy gifts, may pay our thanks to thy divine majesty; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
