O Lord, who alone givest seed to the sower and bread to the eater, and hast taught us to seek from thee our daily bread: Bless the sowing of the seed, and grant fertility to the soil that receives it; and accept the labours of thy servants, for thy glory and the wellbeing of thy people; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
