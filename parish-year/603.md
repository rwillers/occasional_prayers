Almighty God, who through the preaching of thy servants, the blessed Reformers, hast caused the light of the gospel to shine forth: Grant, we beseech thee, that, knowing its saving power, we may faithfully guard it and defend it against all enemies, and joyfully proclaim it, to the salvation of souls and the glory of thy holy name; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
