O Lord Jesus Christ, who as on this day didst receive the homage of those who hailed thee as their King: Accept, we beseech thee, our praise and adoration, our worship and love; and grant that we, who now confess thee with our lips, may never fail to yield thee the service of our lives; for the honour of thy holy name, who livest and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, world without end.
