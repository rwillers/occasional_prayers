O God, who hast included all the commandments in the one commandment of love, so that if we love not our neighbour we cannot fulfill thy law: We humbly pray thee, create in our hearts such a sincere love of one another, that we may be children of our Father in heaven, and true disciples of thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
