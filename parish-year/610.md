O God, who hast built thy Church through the divers gifts and graces of thy Saints: We give thee humble thanks for all good women \[especially this day for thy servant N.\]; help us, we beseech thee, to follow their steps, and fill our hearts with love to thee, and of others for thy sake; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
