Almighty God, who hast given thine only Son to die for us: Grant that we who have been united in the communion of his most precious Body and Blood may be so cleansed from our past sins, and so strengthened to follow the example of his most holy life, that we may hereafter enjoy everlasting fellowship with thee in heaven, through him who loved us and gave himself for us, the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
