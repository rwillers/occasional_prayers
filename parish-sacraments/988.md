O Almighty God, whose blessed Son did lay his hands upon the sick and heal them: Grant, we beseech thee, to this person, upon whom we now lay our hands in his name, refreshment of spirit, and, if it be thy holy will, perfect restoration to health; through the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord.
