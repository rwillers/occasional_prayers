O Heavenly Father, we thank thee that thou hast been pleased to deliver this thy servant and to give her a healthy living child.  We pray that thou wouldest keep her safe, and grant her and her husband grace to bring up their child in thy steadfast fear and love; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
