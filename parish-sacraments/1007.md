O God, the maker and redeemer of all believers: Grant to the soul of thy servant N. all the unsearchable benefits of thy Son’s passion; that in the day of his appearing he, and all the faithful departed, may be manifested as thy children; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord, who liveth and reigneth with thee and the Holy Ghost, one God world without end.
