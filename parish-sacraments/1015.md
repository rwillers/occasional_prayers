Into thy hands, O Lord, we commend the souls of thy servants, as unto a faithful Creator and most loving Redeemer; beseeching thee of thy great mercy to fulfill in them the purpose of thy love, and to bring us with them to the joy of thy heavenly kingdom; through the merits of thy Son our Saviour Jesus Christ.
