O Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ, who didst share at Nazareth the life of an earthly home: Reign, we beseech thee, in the home of these thy servants as Lord and King; give them grace that they may minister to others as thou didst minister to men; and grant that by deed and word they may be witnesses of thy saving love to those amongst whom they live; for thy holy name’s sake, who lives and reignest with the Father and the Holy Spirit, one God, World without end.
