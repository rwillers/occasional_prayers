Almighty and most merciful Father, without whose help we cannot do anything as we ought: We pray that, as thou hast brought these persons together by thy providence, thou wilt enrich them by thy grace, that they may enter into the marriage covenant as in thy sight, and truly keep the vows they are about to make; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
