title: 'Confirmation — Those Preparing for Confirmation (6)'

tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]

Grant, O Lord, that all who shall kneel before thee to receive thy blessing in the laying on of hands may be strengthened with the Holy Ghost the Comforter, and daily increase in thy Holy Spirit more and more until they come unto thy everlasting kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
