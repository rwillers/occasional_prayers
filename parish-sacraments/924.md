Lord Christ, our living Saviour, who in thy risen power didst make thyself known to thy disciples in the breaking of bread: Grant to us, as we now approach the holy mysteries of thy Body and Blood, a fresh manifestation of thy grace and glory, that in fellowship with thee and one another we may gain refreshment of spirit and renewal of strength; for thy holy name’s sake.
