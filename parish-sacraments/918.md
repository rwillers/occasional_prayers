Grant, we pray thee, O Father, that we and all who meet at thy table may come with true repentance, steadfast purpose, living faith, and a thankful remembrance of the death and resurrection of thy dear Son; so that our bodies and souls may be preserved unto everlasting life, and we may feed on him in our hearts by faith with thanksgiving; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
