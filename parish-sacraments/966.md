O Thou who settest the solitary in families and sendest thy peace upon the households of the faithful: Grant thy blessing, we pray thee, to us and to the members of our families; that in lives made strong for thy service, and homes sanctified by thy presence, we may show forth thy glory before the world; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
