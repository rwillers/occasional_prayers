O God, who in these holy mysteries hast vouchsafed to feed us with the flesh and blood of thy dear Son: We beseech thee that all who faithfully partake of the same may grow up in the communion of the body of Christ, and finally attain to the glory of the resurrection; through the merits of the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
