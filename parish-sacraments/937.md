O God of love, who in baptism hast received us into the congregation of Christ’s flock, and hast sealed us as thine own: Grant that we may never be ashamed to confess the faith of Christ crucified, but may fight manfully under his banner against sin, the world, and the devil, and may continue his faithful soldiers and servants unto our lives’ end.
