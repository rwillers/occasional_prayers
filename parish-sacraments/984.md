Almighty, everliving God, maker of all mankind: We beseech thee to have mercy upon this thy servant in his affliction.  Give him grace to take his sickness with patience and courage; and grant that, if it be thy gracious will, he may recover his bodily health, and serve thee henceforth in newness of life; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
