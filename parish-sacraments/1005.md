Most merciful Father, who hast been pleased to take unto thyself the soul of this thy servant \[child\]: Grant to us, who are still in our pilgrimage and who walk as yet by faith, that, having served thee with constancy on earth, we may be joined hereafter with thy blessed saints in glory everlasting; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
