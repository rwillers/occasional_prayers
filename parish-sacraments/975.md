O God, our heavenly Father, who hast blessed thy servants with the gift of a child: Grant, we beseech thee, that they may show their love and thankfulness to thee in so ordering their home, that by their example and teaching they may guide their child in the way of righteousness, to thy great glory; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
