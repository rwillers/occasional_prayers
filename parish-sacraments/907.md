Lord this is thy feast,<br>prepared by thy longing,<br>spread at thy command,<br>attended at thine invitation,<br>blessed by thine own word,<br>distributed by thine own hand,<br>the undying memorial of thy sacrifice upon the cross,<br>the full gift of thine everlasting love,<br>and its perpetuation till the end of time.<br><br>Lord, this is Bread of heaven, Bread of life,<br>that, whoso eateth, shall never hunger more.<br>And this, the Cup of pardon, healing, gladness, strength,<br>that, whoso drinketh, thirsteth not again.<br><br>So may we come, O Lord, to thy table.<br>Lord Jesu, come to us.
