source_order: 1014

Almighty God, whose love is over all thy works in this and every world: Into thy hands we commit the souls of those whom thou hast taken out of our sight; beseeching thee to grant to them the unutterable joys of thine eternal kingdom, and to those who mourn them grace to abide thy will in fortitude of spirit and in patient faith; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
