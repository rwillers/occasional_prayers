Almighty Father, whose grace is sufficient for the needs of all thy children: Hear us as we commend to thee those who in confirmation have been admitted into the full communion of thy Church.  Establish them in thy faith and fear; bind them ever more closely to thee in the fellowship of thy people; and keep them steadfast to the end; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
