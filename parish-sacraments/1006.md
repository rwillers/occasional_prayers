---
title: 'The Departed — The Faithful Departed (1)'
attribution: St. Ignatius Loyola
layout: page
tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]
source_order: 1006
---

Receive, O Lord, in tranquillity and peace, the souls of thy servants who, out of this present life, have departed to be with thee.  Grant them rest, and place them in the habitations of life, the abodes of blessed spirits; and give them the life that knoweth not age, the good things that pass not away; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
