O God, who didst choose Blessed Mary to be the mother of thy Son Jesus Christ: Prepare the heart and mind and body of this woman, that she may be worthy to be the mother of the child thou wilt give her, and that she may rejoice in her motherhood, as Mary did with Jesus Christ our Lord.
