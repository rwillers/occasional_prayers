Most gracious God, who art at this time calling us to prepare ourselves for coming to thy holy table: Grant us the assurance of thy pardoning love, and give us grace from the heart freely to forgive one another.  Accept us as we dedicate ourselves to thee; and grant that, feeding on Christ by faith, we may be strengthened by thy Holy Spirit, and enabled to live in thy fellowship, now and for evermore.
