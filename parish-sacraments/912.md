Grant us, O Lord, the help of thy grace, that at this holy sacrament we may bring all our thoughts and desires into subjection to thy holy will, and may offer our souls and bodies as a living sacrifice to thee, in union with the perfect sacrifice of thy Son, our Saviour Jesus Christ.
