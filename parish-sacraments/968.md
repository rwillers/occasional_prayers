We thank thee, O God, for the children who have grown up in our homes.  Keep them faithful, we pray thee, to thyself, and to us, and to one another.  Let thy love protect those who are absent from us; bless those who are married, and fill their homes with peace; and make us all to be one in the joy and service of thy greater family; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
