title: 'Marriage — At the Publication of Banns (3)'
layout: page
tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]
source_order: 954
---

Almighty Father, who hast hallowed the ordinance of marriage to the blessing of mankind: Give thy grace to these thy servants who are to be joined together as husband and wife, that they may be faithful in love to one another, and live in obedience to thy will; for the sake of Jesus Christ our Lord.
