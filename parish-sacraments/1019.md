source_order: 1019

Have compassion, O Lord, upon all who are mourning for those dear to them, and upon all who are lonely and desolate.  Be thou their comforter and friend; give them such earthly help and consolation as thou seest to be best for them; and grant them a fuller knowledge and realization of thy love; for thy holy name’s sake.
