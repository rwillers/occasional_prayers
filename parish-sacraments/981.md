title: 'The Sick — Visitation of the Sick (1)'
attribution: Canadian Prayer Book

tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]
source_order: 981
---

O Lord Jesu Christ, thou great physician: Look with thy gracious favour upon this thy servant; give wisdom and discretion to those who minister to him in his sickness; bless all the means used for his recovery; stretch forth thy hand, and, if it be thy will, restore him to health and strength, that he may live to praise thee for thy goodness and thy grace; to the glory of thy holy name.
