O God my Father, I thank thee that thou hast welcomed me into thy family and made me thy child.<br>I thank thee for sending thy dear Son to bear my sins, and to set an example for me to follow in my daily life.<br>Give me courage in my confirmation to confess him as my Saviour and Lord, and not to be ashamed of him.<br>Fill me with thy Holy Spirit, that he may strengthen me to overcome temptation, and at all times to be a good soldier of Jesus Christ.<br>Make me thine for ever, and help me to grow steadily in the knowledge of thee, and in the love of thy holy Word.<br>Show me how to introduce my friends to thee, that together we may follow thee all our days; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
