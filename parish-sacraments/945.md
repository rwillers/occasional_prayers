O Lord our God, we commend to thee those who are about to seek thy grace and to renew their vows in confirmation.  Let thy fatherly hand, we beseech thee, ever be over them; let thy Holy Spirit ever be with them; daily increase in them thy manifold gifts of grace; and grant that they may continue thine for ever, until they come to thy everlasting kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord.
