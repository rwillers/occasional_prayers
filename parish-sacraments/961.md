---
title: 'Marriage — Dedication of the Ring (1)'
layout: page
tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]
source_order: 961
---

In thy name, O Lord, we hallow and dedicate this ring; that by thy blessing he who gives it and she who wears it may abide together in thy peace, continue together in thy favour, may live together in thy love, and at the last may dwell together in thy eternal kingdom.
