O Lord God, who by thy divine mercy hast called us to be Christians, servants of the Crucified, signed with his cross: Enable us, for the love which he bore unto us, to choose the good and refuse the evil, swerving neither to the right hand nor to the left, under the standard and in the strength of the same our Saviour Jesus Christ.
