---
title: 'Prayers Which May Be Used at the Burial of One Who Has Taken His Own Life (1)'
layout: page
tags: [Sacraments and Ordinances]
source_order: 1024
---

Almighty God, who art ever ready to forgive, and to whom no prayer is made without hope of mercy: Speak to us thy word of consolation as we draw near to thee under the shadow of this great affliction.  Deepen within our hearts the assurance of thy unfailing compassion; deliver us from all bitterness, despair, and doubt of thy love; and grant us to know the peace which passes all understanding, through Jesus Christ our Lord.
