source_order: 1020

O Eternal Father, who didst not spare thine only Son, but gave him to be the Redeemer of mankind: Draw near, we beseech thee, to those who mourn the loss of loved ones.  Comfort them in their loneliness; supply all their need; suffer them never to doubt thy love, but draw them through their sorrow into closer fellowship with thee; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord.
